Grammar (move-attributes.js CASTER): today commitment.movement is the enum none, slowed, rooted and commitment.cooldown is the enum tick, freeze (tick is just normal operation, so it goes away), and NO move or code uses either (the only casterState in the data is iframes on two 4c moves), so replace them with the argument forms: movement = type plus optional t; cooldown = freeze only, with optional id (default all; a move id or list) and optional t (default the parent's endOn time; a number; or "until effect end"). - P3 Recoil: paid = fraction x maxHp, subtracted directly (not through hurt: no invuln, flash, sound or camera kick), floored so HP never drops below 1. The cast is skipped (cooldown not spent) when current HP is at or below paid, and while a Substitute shield is live. cast.paid and effect.max evaluate after recoil through evalFormula (multiply and ref only, no eval, no Function).
- P4 Shield on Player: fields for current value, max, regen per second, time left, and absorbsAll/overflow behavior. In hurt and hurtDot, after the final damage d is computed and before hp is reduced, the shield absorbs min(shield, d). Absorbed amount shows a blue floating number; if the whole hit was absorbed: no sound, no kick, no flash, no post-hit invulnerability. Spill damage continues down the normal path. DoT drains the shield first (status can still apply). Regen ticks in the player's update, caps at max, stops when the shield ends. Shield is cleared on setActive away, on death, and on a new run. Co-op and net serialization are out of scope (local-first).
- P5 Heal over time (Aqua Ring): per second of the effect's `per`, call the player's heal with fraction x maxHp for the effect's duration; a recast refreshes the remaining time and does not stack; it ends on swap-out or faint. ## Risks to tell Jess (not blockers)
- Flamethrower's uptime drops from about 86 to 46 percent and you are slowed while it fires; judge by feel, balance later.
- Substitute refills a 50% max HP shield every second for 4 s, which is close to immunity while it lasts; the 12 s cooldown starting at shield end bounds the uptime. Balance pass.
- Aqua Ring at 100 percent uptime and 6.25% max HP per second is much stronger than legacy's flat 1.6 HP/s.
- Flat damage ignores all multipliers, so Dragon Rage (40) and level damage fall off late.
- Chains with no falloff floor can still hit hard on dense packs, and a 2-enemy ping-pong repeats the same targets by design.
- A Pokemon with Swords Dance in every slot of a team can reach +6 Attack (x4); that is the intended skill expression, tell Jess what you see.
