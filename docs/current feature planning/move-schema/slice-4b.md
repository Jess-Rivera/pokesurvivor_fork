## Objective
Shapeless self casts (stat, heal over time, recoil plus shield), the special handlers (fixedDamage, levelDamage, statusBonus, chainDamageFalloff, setWeather) and sequential chain hits.

## Decisions (Jess, 2026-10-27)
- S1 Substitute: a 4 s shield on a 12 s cooldown, and the cooldown does not start until the shield is gone (expired, broken, or ended by a swap or faint). Uses the caster-state cooldown freeze (S13) with id = substitute and t = until its shield ends. The cast is skipped while a shield is live and when current HP is at or below the cost (recoil never kills). The shield lives on the active Pokemon and ends on swap or faint. A fully absorbed hit shows a number but gives no hurt sound, camera kick or post-hit invulnerability; spill damage does. Refill stays as authored (full max per second); balance later. Player has no shield today, so player.js gains one.
- S2 Hail leaves 4b. Jess wants it as a toggle skill: cannot bond with other skills, selecting its slot switches it on and returns you to your previous slot, and it stays on until a rule we settle later. That needs a slot-selection hook, a bond exclusion, an on/off lifecycle that outlives the active slot, weather that does not expire at 60 s, and UI. Parked questions: how it ends, indefinite weather, swap lock, cooldown, bond exclusion. The caster takes snow chip damage (decided). 4b still builds and tests the setWeather handler through a synthetic move. The Hail cd/duration puzzle (review 9.7 item 6) is owned by the Hail item, so Hail's 1.2 override stays untouched.
- S3 Night Shade waits for 4c (persistence). 4b tests levelDamage with a synthetic move in the harness.
- S4 Stat buffs: maxStacks 1 PER POKEMON, not per move id. The application source tag becomes move id plus the casting Pokemon, so three Pokemon that each know Swords Dance stack to +6 (the clamp) while one Pokemon's recast refreshes its own. Benched cooldowns do not tick (existing), so the swap-and-cast play needs each cooldown ready on swap-in. Aqua Ring's heal over time also refreshes on recast and never stacks; default: it ends when the Pokemon is swapped out.
- S5 Flat damage is flat: fixedDamage and levelDamage skip STAB, type, crit, stat stages, weather, item and card multipliers. They still go through Enemy.hurt, so enemy shields, death, popups and kill credit work, with the 1 damage floor.
- S6 Aqua Ring moves from legacy 1.6 HP/s flat to 6.25% max HP per second at 100% uptime (cd 8, duration 8). Accepted as authored; goes on the balance list.
- S7 Chains are sequential. Hop 0 hits the target. Hop k picks the nearest live enemy to the enemy hit at hop k-1, within hopRange, excluding ONLY that previous enemy, so A to B to C to A is legal. It stops early when none. Damage at hop k is the full normal computation for that target, then multiplied LAST by (1 - reduction) to the power k (reduction 0.33: 100, 67, 44.89, 30.07, 20.15). Crit, type, riders and thaw roll per hop. Rider chance is halved per hop (Q10, DECIDED): chance x 0.5^k. All hops resolve in the same step. Each hop draws a short zigzag line. With two enemies in range a chain ping-pongs for all its hops (kept, tested). Chain hits are exempt from oncePerInstance; the initial strike still obeys it.
- S8 Hex: same chain rules. Power is doubled when the hop's target has a major ailment (paralysis, freeze, sleep, burn, poison, toxic; confusion and trap do not count), checked at every hop, doubled inside the power term before the attacker half (not a final x2). The chain reduction applies last. Hex needs a chain-falloff entry added to its payload.
- S9 Thunderbolt overlap: the targeted enemy starts the chain; other enemies inside the strike circle take the strike but do not start their own chain.
- S10 Data meaning change: chainDamageFraction (0.33, "fraction of") becomes a per-hop reduction, renamed so the two meanings cannot be confused. Architecture D13, D15 and R5 rewritten to match (2026-10-27).
- S11 Test sets: G.Debug.newcombat('selfcasts') (Swords Dance, Dragon Dance, Aqua Ring, Substitute) and ('specials') (Dragon Rage, Hex, Thunderbolt, Flamethrower). Scope expansion OK'd: player.js, stages.js, move-handler.js, move-overrides.js, move-attributes.js, debug.js, source JSON plus regenerate, the move_parity hail self-test fix, and weather.js only if needed.
- S12 Berry steal stays a stub; an unknown handler name fails at load.
- S13 Caster-state commitment is built in full in 4b (Jess, option B, 2026-10-27), with arguments and defaults. commitment.cooldown = freeze(id, t) (the only value; no commitment means normal cooldown ticking): id defaults to all cooldowns, or names a move id or a list; t defaults to the parent's endOn time (the instance lifetime), or is a number of seconds, or "until effect end" (for an instant self-cast such as Substitute: the shield's life, ended early by a break, a swap or a faint). While frozen the named timers do not run; they arm when the freeze ends (cd x cdMul from that moment). commitment.movement = (type, t): slowed applies -2 Speed stages to the caster for t (x0.667 on the B = 4 Speed ladder, adding with other Speed stages); rooted sets the caster's move speed to 0 like the enemy trap root. t has the same defaults. Commitments end when t elapses, the parent instance ends, or the caster swaps out or faints. Nothing else in caster state changes (iframes wait for 4c).
- S15 Chain hop order (Jess, 2026-10-27, during the build): fresh first. S7 as written (nearest, excluding only the enemy just hit) made a uniform line ping-pong A, B, A, B instead of going forward, which contradicted the line test. A hop now picks the nearest enemy within hopRange that this chain has NOT hit yet; only when none is in range may it revisit (still never the enemy just hit). The line goes forward (5 enemies), two enemies still ping-pong, one enemy still does not hop, A to B to C to A still happens when nothing new is in range. Ties: lowest id. Architecture D15 and R5 updated.
- S14 Flamethrower (already routed since 3d) gains both commitments with default arguments: slowed and freeze(all) for its 1.0 s lifetime. Its cycle becomes a 1.0 s cone plus the 1.167 s cooldown (about 2.17 s, uptime about 46 percent instead of 86), and you move at x0.667 while it fires. Rehit stays 0.2. It is the 8th canary; the 3d and 4a tests that assume its old cycle are updated. Balance later.

- move-route.js: 21 routed ids. move-render.js: chain bolts (always drawn), a shield ring, a player dev line (HP, shield, stages, commitments). debug.js: newcombat('selfcasts') and newcombat('specials').
