## Plan decisions (recommended; approved with this plan)
- P1 Routed list: add exactly razor-leaf, bonemerang, quick-attack, aerial-ace (28 routed in total after 4c-1's 24). The list stays in js/systems/move-route.js only. Flag off, the game is unchanged.
- P2 Leg-keyed hit records (R2). The instance carries a current leg (0 at spawn). The hit record stores the leg it was made in; eligibility treats a record from an earlier leg as absent (for oncePerInstance and interval alike), so each leg has its own once-per-instance memory. The pierce budget (the struck counter, D16) is also per leg: a new leg resets it. Leg 1 begins at the turnaround (returns) or when the caster arrives back at the origin (repositions). `returnHits: out` means leg 1 never arms the hitbox; `outAndBack` arms it. Chain hits keep their 4b exemption.
- P3 Returns motion (D26). The out leg moves along the heading at moveSpeed until the distance from the cast origin (the owner position at cast) reaches `range`; the turnaround happens in that step (the position is not allowed to overshoot range by more than one step). From the turnaround the velocity points at the owner's current position at moveSpeed, recomputed every step, so a moving owner is chased. `returned` fires when the instance centre is within owner radius plus its own radius, the instance ends with reason returned and emits move:end as usual. Count shapes fan out by `spread` as for other moves and each returns on its own. Returns ignore walls (the data has no wall endOn). The cast gate for `direction` already waits for a live enemy within range; do not change it.
- P4 Knockback `direction: travel` (D33), in move-effects.js only. For an instance with a velocity, the knock direction is the instance's current velocity; with no velocity (static, orbit, teleport) it falls back to away from the instance center, as today. When the knock direction points toward the owner (positive dot product with the vector from the enemy to the owner), clamp the distance to max(0, distance(enemy, owner) minus owner radius minus enemy radius), so an enemy is never pulled past the caster's edge. A knock away from the owner is unclamped. The same helper serves `damage` and `flat`. Unrelated knockbacks (Aerial Ace's 80, riders) are unchanged. Bosses, elites and gym leaders keep whatever resistance Enemy.hurt already applies.
- P5 Repositions geometry (D27, R9). The target point is the nearest enemy's position, captured at cast, distance capped by `range` along the line from the origin (use the global range when the move has none: the resolver already fills it). Walk the line in 8 px steps with World.isFree (radius 0.8 x player radius, same call the legacy dash used) and stop at the last free step, then World.collide on the owner for the final position. The cast gate (R9) already waits for a candidate within range; do not change it.
- P6 Swept reposition (Quick Attack). Phase out: the owner's position is written by the sim every step along the line at dashMult x the owner's final move speed (player speed as used by the walk, including stages and status, read through the same getter, with mvRoot ignored for this read; if no clean getter exists, say so). The hitbox is DISARMED in transit, so a decoy on the path takes nothing. Arrival: on the step the owner reaches the point, arm the hitbox for exactly one collision step, test and apply the normal hit pipeline (nearest to the arrival point first, ties by id), then pierce exhaustion disarms it without ending the instance (R1, D16). Phase back (returnToOrigin): the owner travels back to the origin at the same speed, leg 1 begins at arrival, `returned` ends the instance when the owner is within 1 step of the origin (snap to the exact origin). If the target died while the dash was running the owner still goes to the captured point and returns. If every enemy is dead at arrival the hit finds nothing and the owner returns.
- P7 Teleport reposition (Aerial Ace). At cast the owner is placed instantly at the target point (P5), the 46 px hitbox is live from activation for the instance lifetime (endOn time 0.1), the normal hit pipeline runs once, no return (returnToOrigin false). Knockback 80 pushes away from the instance centre (landing point) as today. Confusion rolls through the existing 4a ailment applier.
- P8 Caster state i-frames (D2) and the dash lock (D3). While a reposition instance lives, each step raise owner.invuln to at least the casterState iframes value (so the whole dash is covered); at the instance end the last raise leaves the 0.3 s tail. Do not lower an existing invuln. For the swept dash, set mvRoot true at activation and clear it at the instance end (also clear it on owner death, swap away, run restart and any early end; the 4b commitment root clears in setActive and death, mirror that and check the two do not fight, since they share the flag: use a counter or a source-tagged set if one root can end while the other lives, and say how you did it). The teleport has no lock. hurtDot still ignores invuln.
- P9 Priority: Quick Attack has priority 1 ("always first"). If the cast order in a bonded group sorts by priority already, keep it; if it does not, do not build it here, report it to Jess.
- P10 Render (move-render.js, presentation only): returns shapes drawn as a spinning sprite or the generic shape with a faint trail, the swept dash drawn as a streak from the origin to the owner with an afterimage, the teleport as a short vanish and appear flash at both points, the 0.3 s tail with the owner's usual blink (the existing invuln blink in player.js draws it). The debug overlay prints the instance leg and the owner's invuln value.
- P11 Debug (dev only): G.Debug.newcombat('motion') with Razor Leaf, Bonemerang, Quick Attack and Aerial Ace in four slots, a loose crowd of awake slow dummies, a decoy placed on the dash line for Quick Attack on request. The other newcombat modes keep working.

## Risks to tell Jess (not blockers)
- Quick Attack now hits one enemy at the end point instead of everything on the line as the legacy dash did (Q3 decided); it will feel weaker against crowds.
- The caster overlaps the target enemy at the centre landing; i-frames cover the damage, but it looks busy.
- A target behind a wall means the dash stops at the wall and can whiff.
- Bonemerang pulls and pushes enemies; a crowd will bunch along the bone's path. Judge by feel.
- A long run with a fast owner and a short-range bone can end by time (2 s) instead of returning; that is allowed.
- The swept dash speed uses the owner's final move speed, so Agility or a slow makes the dash slower or faster by design.
