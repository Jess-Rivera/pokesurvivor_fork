3. THE CARDS (Jess, 2026-10-30)
===============================
  GENERAL
  Type (Fire ...) ....... power, moves of that type; same pool as Damage Up
  Gate Reduction ........ stat stage Gate (SS10)
  Duration Increase ..... stat stage Duration (SS10)
  HP Regen .............. % max HP per s, BENCHED Pokemon only (a reason to swap)
  Vitamins .............. on pick, choose 1 of 3 random stat bonuses (HP, Atk, Def,
                          SpA, SpD, Spe), team-wide; replaces the six stat cards
  Dodge Cooldown ........ DEFERRED to the dodge branch (TODO)

  SPACE
  Area Up (name TBD) .... radRadius (points too: bigger shots), linLength, orbitRadius
  Presence Up ........... radArc (not a point, below 360, capped at 360), linWidth (beams)
  Count Up .............. count, only moves whose copies can spread out
                          (count > 1, a spread, an orbit, an emitter fan): 16 moves (2026-10-31 data)
  Pierce Up ............. pierce, never UNLIMITED: 17 moves (incl. emitted shots, 2026-10-31 data)
  Chain Up .............. chainHops, moves that already chain: 3 moves
  Splash ................ DEFERRED (schema: a card turns splash on)

  DELIVERY
  Reach Up .............. range, AND flight time on moves that travel (so a shot reaches)
  Delay Down ............ delay, with a floor: 1 move today (Rock Slide)
  Speed Up (name TBD) ... moveSpeed, angularSpeed
  Time Up ............... endOn time of PLACED things only (puddles, turrets, trails, Toxic
                          Spikes, Discharge rings): 5 moves; never a shot, never the move's own timer
  Cooldown Down ......... cd; persistent: rehit; emitters: emit every. Never pure buffs
                          (no cd; Gate Reduction is theirs). Absorbs Fire Rate

  PAYLOAD
  Damage Up ............. power > 0
  Knockback Up .......... knockback > 0
  Ailment Chance Up ..... chance points (+10, cap 100), chance below 100
  Heal Up ............... heal amount (Aqua Ring, drains)
  Shield Up ............. shield amount (Substitute)
  Recoil Down ........... recoil, floor 0: 1 move today (Substitute; its shield stays full size)

  RETIRED on the new path: Buff Stacks, the 14 kind-damage cards, Size, Arc, Width,
  Shot Range, the old Duration, Projectile Speed, Fire Rate, the six stat cards.
