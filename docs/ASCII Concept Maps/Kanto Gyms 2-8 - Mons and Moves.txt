KANTO GYMS 2-8: MONS AND MOVES AS BUILT (generated 2026-11-05 from js/data/gyms.js and G.MoveDefs)
================================================================================
Purpose: a working sheet for authoring upgrade cards on gyms 2 to 8 (Brock loop 1 is the model:
  docs/BOSS-AUTHORING.md "Cards"). Gym 1 (Brock) is omitted. Nothing here is a decision.

HOW TO READ
  Loop N fields the first N slots at once; slot K enters at loop K. A set applies from its loop key
  until the next key (a loop with no set keeps the last earlier one).
  form    how the leader casts it (G.Gyms.formOf): melee cone, nova ring, shot, strike, beam, buff, heal,
          dash, returns, multi (burst), trail, delayed. "??" = no gym form (should never appear).
  bp      base power per hit.   cast = bp x hits per cast (x2 = hits).   cd = cooldown in s, already divided by
          the slot tempo; "Gate" = pure buff cast on its Gate, no cooldown.   P/s = cast / cd, the tools-only
          D37 proxy (combat-architecture:D37), NOT damage dealt: real damage scales with the mon level, stats
          and the player defense (ratio path), so compare moves with each other only.
  reach   px from the leader centre (G.Gyms.reachOf); the far stance holds 170 to 300 px.
  status  riders: ailment id chance%, flinch %, foe/self stat stages, heal, knockback px.
  weight  WEIGHTING IS GONE since slice 7a: no per-move weights. Every move autocasts; THE PICK scores
          freshness (seconds since last tell / cd) x fit (0.5 for a shot or strike while the player is inside
          close reach) x opportunity (x1.5 Hex vs a major ailment, x0.75 ailment rider vs one already on,
          x1.25 foe stat drop not yet on the player). 'share' below is an ESTIMATE of how the casts split among
          the set's attacks, taken as proportional to 1/cd. Ties break melee, nova, strike, shot, list order.
  hp<     the move joins the mon only below that fraction of max HP.
  cards   the LEADER cards, gained per loop and pooled (G.Gyms.cardsFor); every fielded mon holds the whole pool. Shown per gym. Move numbers below are BASE (no cards folded).
  lv      the gym level = run level when the gate spawns + 4. Stage bands (TUNE): 2-stage line final at 35, 3-stage mid 20, final 35.
