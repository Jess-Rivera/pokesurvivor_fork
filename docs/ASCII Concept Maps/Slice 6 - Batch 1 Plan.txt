1. THE FAIRY WIND PATTERN (what String Shot and Bubble Beam switch to)
================================================================================
  today:   [root shot x N] all leave the same frame        -> stacked
  after:   [root: invisible emitter at you, aims, 'cast' trigger]
               | every E s, N times
               v
           [child shot]  [child shot]  [child shot] ...    -> a line
           gap in px = E x moveSpeed

  move          shots  moveSpeed  every E   gap      emitter life   spread
  ------------  -----  ---------  --------  -------  -------------  ----------
  string-shot     2      320      0.11 s    35 px    0.4 s          0 (line)
  bubble-beam     7      330      0.075 s   25 px    0.6 s          0.12 -> 0
  (fairy-wind     3      340      0.08 s    27 px    0.4 s   unchanged, ref)

  Each child keeps the old shot's body (7 px, pierce 0), speed, flight time and
  payload (String Shot: Speed -2 + trap; Bubble Beam: damage + 10% Speed -1).
  The cd stays on the root (String Shot 1.1 override; Bubble Beam 0.875 formula).

2. BUBBLE BEAM WOBBLE (part of D-BB)
================================================================================
  side-to-side speed peak = 2 pi x frequency x amplitude

                 amplitude  frequency  side speed  forward  wave length
  today            18 px     3 Hz       339 px/s   330      110 px   <- wobble = travel
  proposed         10 px     1.5 Hz      94 px/s   330      220 px

  Why amplitude drops too: at 1.5 Hz a bubble near a close enemy sits at its widest
  swing; 18 px off-axis is outside an enemy body (7 + 10 = 17 px) so a close enemy
  would be missed. 10 px always stays inside.
  Each bubble starts its own wave when it is emitted, so the 7 read as a snake.
