Brock
You: "closing the distance too much for his identity"

Why he walks in

  • His range is 150 px. Maintain (his top goal) means "land a hit from 150 px", so whenever you stand farther off, Maintain's plan starts with walking in to 150.
  • Walking in costs him the normal amount (approach x1), and so does Dash in.
  • Rock Throw reaches about 370 px (before his velocity cards), so he never needs to come that close to hit you.
Misty
You: "bopping around and not fighting"

Why she keeps moving

  • Kite weight 1.5: Kite means "land a hit from outside your reach", so whenever you step in, her best plan starts by backing out again.
  • Backing off is discounted (retreat x0.8), so the move away is cheap.
  • risk 1.1 makes every hit she might take count 10% more, which pushes her further toward running.
