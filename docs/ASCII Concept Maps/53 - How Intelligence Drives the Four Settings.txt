=====================================================================================
1. THE PIPE: ONE NUMBER IN, FIVE SETTINGS OUT (each fight, at fight start)
=====================================================================================

  gyms.js  ai.intelligence  { base, growth }   or a plain number
                |
                v
  intelligenceForLoop = base + growth x (loop - 1)    held inside 0..1
                |
     +-----------+------------+-------------+--------------+--------------+
     v           v            v             v              v
  misjudge    reflex       dodgeRead     memoryUse      aimError
  (plans)     (reacting)   (noticing)    (learning you) (rating its own shots)
     |           |            |             |              |
  authored directly? -> that value wins; its curve and growth are skipped (per setting)

=====================================================================================
2. THE CURVES: two straight lines that meet at the default (intelligence 0.5)
=====================================================================================

  Why two lines: one straight line through today's value at 0.5 cannot be wide at the dumb end AND
  short of perfect at the top (it would run past perfect). So each setting has three points: the dumb
  end (0), today's value (0.5, so Misty and Brock keep what was feel-tested), and the top (1), with a
  straight line between each pair.

  intelligence   0.00      0.25      0.50       0.75      1.00      never reaches
  -------------  --------  --------  ---------  --------  --------  ------------------------------
  misjudge       0.30      0.20      0.10       0.07      0.04      0 (always misreads a little)
  reflex         0.300 s   0.225 s   0.150 s    0.135 s   0.120 s   0.10 s (the hard floor)
  dodgeRead      2         4         6          7         8         9 or 10 of every 10
  memoryUse      0.15      0.33      0.50       0.60      0.70      an exact cooldown read
  aimError       40 px     25 px     10 px      7 px      4 px      0 (a perfect read of its odds)
                                     ^
                    the default leader: today's reflex, dodgeRead, memoryUse.
                    Rule changes at the default: misjudge 0 -> 0.1 (approved), aimError 0 -> 10 px (new).

  fairness ceilings stay outside intelligence's reach:
     predictCap (acts on a pattern at most 7 in 10)   cooldown window floor 0.2 s   tells   stall guards

=====================================================================================
3. WHAT EACH ONE DOES IN A FIGHT
=====================================================================================

  MISJUDGE  how badly it reads what a plan will cost
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    each plan, each think tick:  misreadCost = cost x (1 + misjudge x noiseDraw),  noiseDraw seeded in -1..1
    two plans for one goal, true costs 0.050 and 0.054:
       misjudge 0.30   seen as 0.035..0.065 and 0.038..0.070   picks the worse one often, even the wrong goal
       misjudge 0.10   seen as 0.045..0.055 and 0.049..0.059   picks the worse one now and then
       misjudge 0.04   seen as 0.048..0.052 and 0.052..0.056   almost always right, still not certain

  REFLEX  delay from noticing your shot to answering it (a threat lands within 0.6 s)
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
       0.6 s ---------------------------------------------------> impact
       |-- reflex --|-- time left to get out of the way -------|
         0.30 s       0.30 s       intelligence 0    (a Rock Throw-speed shot covers 113 px meanwhile)
         0.15 s       0.45 s       intelligence 0.5  (57 px, today)
         0.12 s       0.48 s       intelligence 1    (45 px)

  DODGE READ  how many of every 10 threats it notices at all (shuffle bag, seeded)
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
       intelligence 0     [x x . . . . . . . .]  2 of 10
       intelligence 0.5   [x x x x x x . . . .]  6 of 10   (today)
       intelligence 1     [x x x x x x x x . .]  8 of 10   always misses some
    an unread threat is never dodged, however good the reflex.

  MEMORY USE  how fast it learns your cooldowns
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    its guess at a move's cooldown starts at 0.2 .. 2.0 s; you recast after 1.2 s (a clean gap):
       memoryUse 0.15   0.35 .. 1.88 s    still mostly guessing
       memoryUse 0.50   0.70 .. 1.60 s    today
       memoryUse 0.70   0.90 .. 1.44 s    knows your timing after a few casts, never exactly
    your counter: cycle slots, swap. (memory across fights, learnRate, is separate)

  AIM ERROR  how well it rates its OWN chance to hit you (planning only)
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    the planner's hit chance = half width / (half width + your escape + aimError)
    example, a shot with half width 25 px that you can sidestep by 60 px:
       aimError  0 px   rates it 29%   (today)
       aimError  4 px   28%            (intelligence 1)
       aimError 10 px   26%            (default)
       aimError 40 px   20%            (intelligence 0: thinks its shots miss, plays timid)
    It does NOT move where its shots go: your moves auto-target, and a leader casts through your
    cast path, so there is no aim to throw off.
