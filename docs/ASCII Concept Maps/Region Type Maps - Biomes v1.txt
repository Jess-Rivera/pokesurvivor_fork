APPROVED CAST FILL ORDER (per map, per BST tier, 9 species, mix 1-2 S / 3-5 M / 3-5 R)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. on-type,  own gen                 (has a map type)
  2. on-type,  earlier gens            only if step 1 leaves a family short
  3. off-type, own gen    \  fills the MISSING FAMILIES only
  4. off-type, earlier    /  (same idea as the gym off-type fill rule)
  5. ??? lower BST tier                today's poolFor borrow; see D5 below
  Kanto T6 needs step 5 no matter what: only 7 wild Gen-1 species sit in 530-620.

OPEN DECISIONS (each changes spawn behavior; not built)
-------------------------------------------------------
  D4  type match: either slot (hordes today) | primary only
  D5  step 5 order: lower-tier on-type BEFORE off-type (theme wins, enemies weaker)
                    | off-type BEFORE lower tier (power ramp wins, theme thins)
  D6  spawn weight: on-type species weighted up vs off-type fill (theme reads stronger
      on screen even when 3 of 9 cast slots are off-type) | equal weights
  D7  biome set: one shared set for all regions (names can be regional skins later)
                 | hand-authored 9 per region
  D8  hordes on type maps: map types only | any type in the tier | off-type hordes
      as the "something new" beat
  D9  Kanto T6: accept off-theme | cap Kanto at T5 cast + elites | fold into D5
