2. THE BIG IDEA: A LEADER IS A PLAYER, DRIVEN BY AN AI THROUGH YOUR CONTROLS
=====================================================================================

  you:     keyboard  --+                              +--> same 7 calls (G.PlayerEntity.IDLE):
                       +--> Controller --> Body ...   |    axis  dodge  shake  swapSlot  swapDir  slots  cycles
  leader:  G.LeaderAI -+                              |
                                                      +--> no hidden powers: anything a player can't do is an
                                                           AUTHORED difference in gyms.js (tells, firstWait, ...)

  It sees only the SCREEN (positions, shots, tells, HP bars, types, statuses, rosters, moves once cast).
  Never: live cooldown timers, cards, IVs, EVs, nature, exact stats, RNG, your next input.

3. ONE FRAME, FOUR ROLES (yours and the leader's run the same order)  [#40, #41, #43]
=====================================================================================

  +-----------+     +------------+     +--------------+     +-----------------------------+
  |  DECIDER  | --> | CONTROLLER | --> |     BODY     | --> |           SIMPASS           |
  | decides,  |     | carries,   |     | holds state, |     | resolves what was accepted  |
  | no rules  |     | no filter  |     | accept or    |     | + timers, DoT, commitments, |
  |           |     |            |     | refuse only  |     |   other entities' hits      |
  +-----------+     +------------+     +--------------+     +-----------------------------+
   you: your head    your hands         the mon on field     G.MoveRoute.tick (yours)
   AI:  G.LeaderAI   AI's controller    G.GymLeader          G.GymLeader.SimPass
        .think                          acceptsAttack:       the tell (authored), every cast
                                        stun, flinch hold,   through YOUR cast gate (#47a,
                                        firstWait            G.MoveSim.gateOpen, 0.05 s retry)

  A refused request passes NOTHING on.  A stun refuses move/dodge/new attack only; slot presses,
  swaps and timers keep running; a stun mid wind-up cancels it.
  Order checked step by step: docs/proposals/player-move-trace-harness.js

5. GOALS (9, fixed in code; leaders only weight them)       ACTIONS (10 verbs, fixed in code)
=====================================================================================

  goal        target (an outcome)            profile          verb               tag
  ----------  -----------------------------  ---------------  -----------------  --------
  Endurance   land a hit                     patient          Close to reach X   approach
  Maintain    land a hit from `range`        neutral          Hold at range R    position
  Pressure    land a hit                     rushes           Back off           retreat
  Setback     land a status / lower a stage  neutral          Dash in (X)        dash
  Kite        hit from outside your reach    very safe        Wait for a window  wait
  Buff        gain +1 stage                  safe             Draw your shot M   approach
  Bait        hit while your best move cools very safe, PARKED Dodge your shot M dodge
  Finish      KO your active mon             all in           Use slot X         from data
  Conserve    recover heal / cd / dodge      safest           Swap to mon Y      swap
                                                              Recover            wait
  ONE CURRENCY: HP traded, as a fraction of the other side's max HP.
  cost = damage taken (danger x time, x risk) - damage dealt (opportunity x hit chance) + time, x tag multiplier

7. FAIRNESS (smart, not oppressive)
=====================================================================================

  human limits   reflex delay, think tick, aimError, dodge only after reflex + with a charge
  prediction cap a believed pattern acts at most predictCap in 10 (default ~70%)  -> you can bait it
  stall guards   minField before a voluntary swap, swapLimit per fight
  tells          on by default (authored `tells`)
  NO RUBBER BAND never touches stats, HP, damage, tempo, cooldowns; confidence changes behavior only
  deterministic  rates are counters/bags, randomness seeded per fight; harness fixes the seed
