WHERE THE NUMBERS COME FROM (found in code, 2026-11-12)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 a hit on a wild enemy or elite   move-effects.js damage() and flat(): they know the CASTER, the move id,
                                  the crit roll and the type multiplier, then call the caster's onDealt.
                                  -> onDealt gains (move id, crit, type mult); the enemy remembers its
                                     last hitter. This unblocks A1 (per-move damage) and B2 (crits) later.
 a hit on a gym leader            move-effects.js hurtPlayer(): same, the owner is known.
 a kill                           enemy.js payKill(): reads the enemy's last hitter (D1a).
 a wild enemy's burn / poison     enemy.js applyBurn / applyPoison take NO source today, and takeDot
                                  credits nobody. -> they gain an "inflicted by" (the caster), kept while
                                  the state lasts (the strongest source already wins today; P6).

DECISIONS  (D1, P5 are RULES: they change what gets counted; flagged on their own)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 D1 RULE  per-Pokemon kill credit
          (a) the Pokemon whose hit finished it, a DoT tick finishing it counts for the inflicter <- rec
              (matches the damage rule: a move still flying after a swap credits its caster)
          (b) whoever is on the field when it dies
          The TEAM kill count does not change either way.
 P5 RULE  DoT damage on enemies (burn, poison, toxic)
          today it is counted NOWHERE: not in Damage, not in the DPS panel, not in the team's damage.
          (a) credit it to the inflicter: it joins that Pokemon's Damage and the team's DPS <- rec
              VISIBLE CHANGE: the DPS panel's number and line go up for status-heavy teams.
          (b) count it only on its own "DoT dmg" row, Damage and DPS unchanged
 P1 layout   (a) Run Stats grows to 360 px, Cards Taken shrinks to 240 and scrolls <- rec (spec allows the
                 split)   (b) keep 200 px, Run Stats scrolls too
 P2 rows     the 12 above <- rec. Drop candidates if it feels busy: Share, DoT dmg, Down.
 P3 statuses (a) one total row, per kind on hover <- rec   (b) one row per kind landed (box gets taller)
 P4 numbers  (a) compact past 9,999: 41.2k, 1.3M (Vampire Survivors' results do the same with SI
                 prefixes) <- rec   (b) full numbers, smaller text
 P6 who inflicted a status that is already on: the source with the strongest mul takes it over (it already
             wins the damage); a tie keeps the first <- rec. Not a rule change: only who gets the credit.
