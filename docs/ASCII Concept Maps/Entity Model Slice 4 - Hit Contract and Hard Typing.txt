 4a. TODAY: TWO HIT PATHS, PICKED BY THE ATTACKER
=====================================================================================

  attacker -> target       entry                      attacker half            defender half
  ----------------------   ------------------------   ----------------------   --------------------------------
  you -> wild (move)       move-effects damage()      A = your moveFactor      (A/Def + 2) x STAB x 1.0 (DMG_SCALAR)
                           -> Enemy.hurt(final dmg)                            x type x items x crit x chain
  leader -> you (move)     move-effects hurtPlayer()  A = leader factor        (A/Def + 2) x 0.5 (ENEMY_DMG_SCALAR)
                           -> Player.hurt(A, opts)       x STAB (folded in A)  x type x weather x berry x cushion
                                                                               x crit x reduction, shield, sash
  wild -> you (contact,    combat.js, hazards.js      A = enemyAttackFactor    same as the row above
   shot, hazard)           -> Player.hurt(A, opts)       (no STAB)
  burn/poison on wild      Enemy.dot()                1/16, 1/8, N/16 max HP   Enemy.hurt
  burn/poison on you       Player.statusDot()         G.Status stDot           Player.hurt (pure)
  riders                   APPLY table (wild) vs PL table (you), picked by owner.foes = "the attacker is a leader"

  The problem at slice 5: a leader becomes a Player entity, and you hit it. Today's paths would
  pick the WILD rider table (you are not a leader), give it no defense of its own, and STAB and
  the two scalars sit in different halves, so the numbers come out wrong either way.

 4a. DECISIONS
=====================================================================================
  H1. DECIDED (Jess, 2026-11-06): STAB works everywhere, from anyone to anyone, at the mainline
      spot: (A/D + 2) x 1.5 when the move or attack type is one of the ATTACKER's types.
      4a carries stab in the hit but keeps today's values (neutral). The flip is in 4b, the
      balance commit, with hard typing. What it changes, per attacker:

        attacker -> target   today                        after (4b)
        ------------------   --------------------------   ----------------------------------
        you -> wild          (A/D + 2) x 1.5              same
        leader -> you        (1.5A/D + 2) x 0.5 (folded)  (A/D + 2) x 1.5 x 0.5: +0.5 HP/hit
        wild -> you          (A/D + 2) x 0.5, no STAB     x 1.5 on EVERY wild hit, see S1
        you -> leader        (slice 5)                    the mainline formula, like wilds
        flat damage          no STAB                      no STAB (see H5; mainline agrees)

  S1. DECIDED (Jess, 2026-11-06): B. Wild STAB on, ENEMY_POWER 40 -> 27.
      Found: a wild enemy's contact AND its shots use its first type (enemy.js atkType =
      types[0]). So with STAB everywhere, every wild hit on you is STAB: x1.5 damage taken from
      the whole horde, on top of hard typing.
      A: take the x1.5. The game gets harder from minute one.
      B (proposed): STAB on, and the wild placeholder Power (ENEMY_POWER, stats.js, marked
         TEMPORARY until enemies draw real moves) goes 40 -> 27. Worked by hand:
           today  (A/D + 2) x 0.5
           B      (0.675 A/D + 2) x 1.5 x 0.5 = (1.01 A/D + 3) x 0.5   about +0.5 HP per hit
         The rule is real STAB; the horde's damage stays about where it is.
      C: STAB on for moves only (you and leaders); wild contact and shots stay without it.
      Checked 2026-11-06: ENEMY_POWER is read only by the wild attack number (enemy.js
      atkFactor). That one number feeds wild contact, wild shots and the hazards wild enemies
      leave, and Elites (atkFactor0 x power x enrage), so all of them move together. Leaders
      pass their own move power (boss-ai.js; its fallback is a literal 40), so B never
      touches them. A hazard gets STAB only when its type is the enemy's own type.

  H2. DECIDED: the 0.5 is the human's damage-taken scale. Every other target takes 1.0.

  H3. DECIDED (Jess, 2026-11-06): the side effects of a hit ("riders") are picked by the TARGET.
      A move hit has a damage part and side effects: flinch, knockback, burn or poison, stat
      drops, berries. Today there are two side-effect rule sets, and the code picks one by
      asking "is the ATTACKER a gym leader?":

        side effect    rules for a hit ON YOU             rules for a hit ON A WILD
        -----------    --------------------------------   --------------------------------
        flinch         at most one roll per move/0.5 s    every hit rolls
        knockback      only push moves (Roar) move you    every hit can shove it
        burn, poison   G.Status (your DoT, berries)       its own burn and poison fields
        Fissure KO     never                              can KO a plain wild

      Today that question gives the right answer by luck: only leaders hit you with moves,
      only you hit wilds. At slice 5 a leader is a Player entity and YOU hit it. "Is the
      attacker a leader?" says no, so the leader would get the WILD rules: shoved by every
      hit, flinched every hit, burned on fields it does not have.
      Proposed: ask "what is the TARGET?" instead. A Player entity (you, a leader) gets the
      left column, a wild entity the right. No change today; correct at slice 5.

  H4. DECIDED: one DoT entry per family (takeDot); the two state models stay apart.

  H5. DECIDED (Jess, 2026-11-06): flat damage stays outside the hit contract (I5 may refine it).
      The contract is for ATTACKS: damage computed from the attacker's stats and divided by
      the target's defense (an "attack factor"). Those get STAB, type, crit and defense.
      FLAT damage is a fixed HP number: no attacker stat, no defense. It just subtracts.

        stays flat (plain hurt), as today        where                 number
        -------------------------------------    -------------------   ----------------------
        lava and other damaging liquids          player.js, enemy.js   share of max HP
        Bomb pickup                              game.js               60 + 14 x level, or KO
        Rocky Helmet (when touched)              combat.js             15 + 4 x level
        altars and chests (poison, blast, trap)  interact.js           fixed or share of HP
        Fissure's one-hit KO                     move-effects.js       the target's HP
        Dragon Rage, Night Shade (fixed moves)   move-effects.js       40, or your level
        hazards with no attack factor            hazards.js            the hazard's dmg
        legendary world bosses' contact          combat.js             their flat dmg

      Why they stay out: there is no attacker half to move, and sending them through the
      contract would mean inventing a defense and STAB for them, which changes the numbers.
      Mainline agrees for the fixed moves (Dragon Rage ignores STAB). Two notes:
       - flat hits on YOU still get the type chart when they have a type (Player.hurt does
         that today), so hard typing reaches them. Legendary boss contact is the main one.
       - world bosses on the flat path are a placeholder; moving them to attacks belongs to
         the WorldBoss plan, not here.

  Today every hit has a floor of 1 damage, in 4 places:
    move-effects.js damage()  Math.max(1, ...) twice      your attacks on wilds
    enemy.js hurt()           Math.max(1, round(amount))  every hit on a wild
    player.js hurt()          Math.max(1, raw)            every hit on you
  An immune hit would still do 1, and no hit shows "no effect" (label() says 'weak' for x0).

  I1. Immunity beats the floor. An x0 hit deals 0, both ways. Non-immune hits keep the
      floor of 1. (Closes the COMBAT-RULES [Open] item "Does immunity override the floor of
      1?", recommended yes there.)

  I2. What an immune hit does.
        part                         proposed
        --------------------------   ---------------------------------------------------
        damage                       0
        side effects                 none: no burn, poison, flinch, knockback, stat drop,
                                     drain heal (mainline: a move that does not affect the
                                     target has no secondary effect)
        on you                       no i-frames, no flash, no hurt sound or camera kick,
                                     Substitute not drained, Focus Sash not used
        geometry                     it still COUNTS as a hit: a shot stops on it (uses
                                     its pierce), a chain can hop from it. No move-sim.js
                                     change. (Alternative: immune targets are see-through
                                     to your shots; needs move-sim.js, a bigger change.)
        pure status moves            unchanged: they keep their own immunity lists
                                     (G.Status, e.g. Electric types and paralysis)

  I3. Bosses that you cannot hurt.
      Found: a fight with nothing to hit it with. A gym leader enrages (you lose, slowly);
      an Elite hunts you (you run); a Ghost horde blocks a Normal-only lead.
      Escape valves today: team swaps, the Move Shop, Power Stone (its type already ignores
      immunity, typeK floors it at 1).
        A  DECIDED: mainline everywhere: x0 on wilds, Elites, leaders, world bosses.
        B  (was proposed) mainline on wilds and gym leaders (you see a gym coming and can plan
           the team), x0.25 floor on Elites and world bosses (they show up unannounced).
        C  x0.25 floor on every boss-class target (QA-12's "boss-immunity rule").

  I4. Feedback. A grey "Immune" text on the target (the status-immunity text already says
      that), at most once per target every 0.5 s so a horde does not spam it. label()
      returns 'immune'. Same on you.

  I5. Fixed-damage moves and immunity (refines H5).

      Today they ignore type entirely (move-effects D12, "ignoresType by construction").
        A  DECIDED: mainline: they check immunity only, still no resist or weakness.
        B  they stay fully typeless (H5 exactly as approved).

=====================================================================================
 RISKS
=====================================================================================
  - 4a: a rider or multiplier lands in a different order (crit before or after defense on
    you, chain falloff last). The parity harness pins each, written before anything moves.
  - 4a: four harness stubs need a takeHit (fixtures only). An expected value changing = stop.
  - 4b: difficulty both ways. Early gyms against a weak lead get harder; double weaknesses
    (Geodude to Water) become one-shots. That is the point, but watch the first gyms.
  - 4b x0, both ways: some of YOUR Pokemon become immune to whole wild families (a Ghost
    takes 0 from Normal and Fighting contact, Flying from Ground, Steel from Poison). That
    is a big defensive swing for those teams; watch it.
  - 4b x0: checked 2026-11-06. Tier 0 wilds (BST 180 to 330, from 0:00) can include Ghosts
    (Gastly 310) in a region that has them. Every starter opens with Vine Whip, Razor Leaf,
    Ember or Water Gun, and Grass, Fire and Water have no immunity in the chart, so a new team
    can hurt everything. The risk returns only for a team built later around Normal moves.
  - 4b x0 with I3 = A: an Elite or world boss immune to your team's types cannot be hurt.
    Escape valves: swap, Move Shop, Power Stone, or run. Watch the first feel tests.
  - 4b: tools/export_data.js publishes the chart for the web pages; it picks up the new
    values on the next publish, no edit.
