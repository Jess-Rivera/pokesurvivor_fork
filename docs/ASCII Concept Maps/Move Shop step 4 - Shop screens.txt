=====================================================================
 2. STOCK
=====================================================================

  Move Trainer   per member: 4 random from its level-up and egg pool,
                 minus moves it knows. Rolled the first time you look at
                 that member, kept for this building: leaving and coming
                 back shows the same 4. A bought move leaves the stock.
                 Fewer than 4 left: show fewer.
  TM Mart        4 random from the whole TM list, once per building.
                 Never sold out: buy one for each member who can learn it.
  Next building pair: everything rolls fresh.

=====================================================================
 3. PRICE  (approved: 5 x world level x character level)
=====================================================================

  world level = the whole number on the HUD's ENEMY LV (top right)
  character level = the run level (the whole team shares it)

  run time   ENEMY LV   your lv   price
  ─────────────────────────────────────
   2:00         4          5       100
   2:00         4          8       160
   6:30        11         12       660
  10:00        17         18     1,530

  Paid on confirm. A forgotten move refunds nothing. Short of Silver:
  the offer shows "can't afford" and cannot be picked.

  WATCH (no change proposed, the numbers are approved):
     income per kill = world level / 10, price = 5 x world level x your lv
     -> world level cancels out:
        moves per shop ~= kills since the last shop / (50 x your lv)
        at lv 12: about 600 kills per move.
     The step 5 bot measures real kill counts before anything is tuned.

=====================================================================
 3b. WHEN YOU HOLD A BOND  (how it works today, plus the shop)
=====================================================================

  Today: a held bond is re-checked EVERY frame of play (game.js), and
  the bond screen opens as soon as it has something to join (2 loose
  moves, or 1 loose move + a bond with room). Learning a move from a
  level-up card is how a held bond usually becomes usable.

  With the shop:

   walk in ─► bond screen already open?  It cannot be: a usable bond
              opens the moment it is usable, and the shop waits for
              any open screen (G3). So a pending bond is ALWAYS
              chosen first, then the shop opens.
       │
       ▼
   shop open ─► buy 1, buy 2 ... (bond screen held back, you may still
       │        be shopping; you pick the bond with your final lineup)
       ▼
   Leave ─► next frame: the bond screen opens if a purchase made the
            bond usable. Pick it as today.

  Side effect worth knowing (no change proposed): a bond JOINS two
  slots into one, so it frees a slot. If you leave, bond, and walk back
  in within the shop's minute, that freed slot takes a purchase without
  forgetting anything.

 5. DECISIONS FOR JESS
=====================================================================

  G1  BONDS ARE FIXED   (new rule)   APPROVED (Jess 2026-11-05, revised)
      A bonded slot can never be changed by the shop: its moves cannot
      be forgotten or replaced. The only way a bond changes is the normal
      bond screen, adding a loose move when you hold a link (as today).
      The forget step lists bond slots greyed out. A member whose 4 slots
      are all bonds cannot buy from the Trainer or the Mart.

  G2  TMs ON THE POKEDEX AND TEAM SCREENS   APPROVED: later, TODO.md
      Today they list level-up and egg moves only, so Draco Meteor (the
      one TM-only move) cannot be found there.
      proposed:  not in step 4. The TM Mart itself shows who can learn
                 each TM. One line in TODO.md.
      alt:       add a "TMs" group to both screens now (smoke-ui changes)

  G3  ENTERING   APPROVED
      proposed:  entering pauses the run; it never opens over another
                 screen, it waits (section 1) and the visit is not used up.

  G4  THE SHOP RULES STILL MARKED PROPOSED IN THE SPEC   APPROVED as listed
      - a known move is not offered; a bought move leaves the stock
      - each member's 4 are rolled once per building (no reroll by
        walking out and in)
      - a TM is usable when it is on the member's TM list or its pool;
        members who can't, or already know it, show as unavailable
      - pay on confirm, no refund for a forgotten move
      - the 2-buff limit applies (a buff may replace a buff)
      - a purchase never changes your active slot
      - fainted members are listed but cannot be shopped for
      - price uses the whole ENEMY LV the HUD shows (section 3)

  (G5 dropped: skills never bond already, bondOptions in player.js skips
   them, and with bonds fixed no skill can reach a bond seat.)
