=====================================================================

    "Ban" (code name Veto), max 3 levels (js/data/upgrades.js: base 300, grow 1.9)
    each level = ban 1 move for THAT species ("it never shows up in
    your cards"); the starting move cannot be banned. Saved per
    species (save.pban), applied at run start (game.js line 240).

  Read today by:  level-up New Move cards (levelup.js)
                  the startMoves perk's extra move (game.js)
  NOT read by:    the Move Trainer stock (step 4)

  STEP 5 removes the New Move cards ──► Ban would then do nothing at
  all in a normal run, on an upgrade players paid for.
