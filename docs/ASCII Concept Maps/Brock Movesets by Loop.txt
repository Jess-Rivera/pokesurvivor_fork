THE RULE (proposed)
================================================================================
Each mon walks its OWN Brock history, oldest/weakest appearance first, one
step per loop. Loop 1 lands on Gen 1 (Brock's L12 Geodude, which matches our
~L12 loop-1 gym level). Loop 6 lands on the PWT tournament sets (his most
optimized teams).

  loop:     1        2        3        4        5        6
  slot 1  [RBY]---[GSC]---[LGPE]--[HGSS-R]-[PWT-K]--[PWT-W]
  slot 2          [GSC]---[HGSS]--[comp]---[comp]---[PWT-W*]
  slot 3                  [GSC]---[HGSS]---[LGPE]---[PWT-K]
  slot 4                           [HGSS]---[LGPE]---[PWT-K]
  slot 5                                    [LGPE]---[PWT-W]
  slot 6                                             [PWT-W]

  source key
  RBY    Red/Blue (Lv12)                 GSC    Crystal gym (Lv41-44)
  HGSS   HeartGold/SoulSilver gym (Lv51-54)
  HGSS-R HGSS rematch, Fighting Dojo (Lv54-61)
  LGPE   Let's Go rematch (Lv56-57)
  PWT-K  Black 2/White 2 PWT, Kanto Leaders (Lv50)
  PWT-W  B2W2 PWT, World Leaders / Type Expert (Lv50)
  comp   COMPOSITE: no single Brock set exists, built from his other canon
  *      move set taken from Brock's Rhyperior (same line, Gen 4 evo)

DESIGN FLAGS (for later, not blocking)
================================================================================
  - Earthquake is on 6 mons by loop 6. Lore-true (it is Brock's whole identity
    past Gen 1) but it means 6 overlapping ground pulses in the brawl.
  - Sandstorm shows up on 3 mons (L4+). Natural fit for a weather-system hook
    (js/systems weather) rather than a per-mon attack.
  - Coverage moves (Fire/Thunder Punch, Blizzard, Ice Beam, Megahorn, Fangs)
    are Brock's canon anti-Water/Grass answers: built-in anti-cheese.
  - Horn Drill (OHKO) and Bide/Rollout/Protect/Endure need a survivor-like
    translation before they can be bosses' moves.
  - Catalog gap: most of these moves are not in js/data/moves.js yet; the gap
    list gets produced when the gym-roster branch is planned.
