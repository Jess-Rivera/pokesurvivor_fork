SLICE 7a: HOW FAR TO SPLIT BOSSES FROM ENEMY.JS  (Jess, 2026-10-31, OPEN)
================================================================================

A boss today is two things glued together:

  BODY  (what makes it a thing in the world)      BRAIN  (what it decides)
  ------------------------------------------      ------------------------
  HP, hurt(), die(), shield, drops                 move pick, range, HP gates
  burn / poison / flinch entry points              wind-up, cast, recovery
  status + stage fields (G.Status, G.Stages)       stance, far/close modes
  sprite animator, hurt numbers, faint fade        enrage, fury, contact move
  a slot in the EnemyMgr spatial grid  <---- every player move finds Brock here

WHY NOT C
================================================================================

  player move hits Brock
        |
        v
  EnemyMgr grid  --> e.hurt()  --> e.applyBurn / applyPoison / applyFlinch
        |                              |
        |                              +--> G.Status, G.Stages read e's fields
        v
  C needs a second copy of every one of these on the Boss class, kept in sync
  forever. A status or damage fix made on one side silently misses the other.
  It also goes against the 2026-10-23 decision: one executor, a boss is just
  another caster (and target).
