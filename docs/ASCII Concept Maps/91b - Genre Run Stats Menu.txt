WHAT THE GENRE ACTUALLY SHOWS  (sourced)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Vampire Survivors  results screen: stage, mode, time survived, gold, level, enemies defeated; per WEAPON
                    its level, damage, time held and DPS, the top damage and top DPS weapon in yellow;
                    every pickup with its count.            https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Results
                    Caveat (players): a late weapon's DPS reads high because enemy density climbs.
                    https://steamcommunity.com/app/1794680/discussions/0/5287792084460039699/
 Brotato            stats screen shows each weapon's damage in the LAST WAVE, so you know what to invest in.
                    https://www.player.one/brotato-update-0616-improves-stats-screen-implements-gladiator-changes-152839
 Halls of Torment   per-map personal records: high score, fastest time, most kills, by map and mode.
                    https://steamcommunity.com/app/2218750/allnews/
 Hades (community)  players ask for a live "my stats right now" screen: attack %, crit, dodge.
                    https://steamcommunity.com/app/1145360/discussions/0/1736636051411523768/

 Pattern: (1) a per-SOURCE damage table with a highlighted MVP, (2) records per map, (3) pickups counted.

FEEL / READABILITY GUARDS  (design reference: choice paralysis, unreadable screens)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 - The pause menu is a glance, not a spreadsheet: Run Info ~12 rows max, Run Stats box 5 to 7 rows.
 - Deep tables (per move per Pokemon, records) live on the end-of-run screen (#92), like VS's results.
 - Show DPS beside total damage, never alone (the VS density caveat): A1 shows damage AND share %.
 - Counters are plain increments at events, never per-frame scans: no perf cost worth measuring.
