ACQUISITION v2: SPEC (final rules only, 2026-11-13)
=====================================================
Canonical spec for how a Pokemon is obtained, and how it evolves past Stage 1.
History and the reasoning behind each rule: "Acquisition v1 - Catch, Candy, Legendaries.txt" (rounds 1 to 35).
Decision: docs/decisions/2026-11-12-acquisition-no-gacha-catch-candy-legendaries.md
Tags: every rule is [Approved], not built (code differs, see TODO), unless marked [Open] or [Parked].
Data facts are from pokedex.js, enemy-classes.js, sprites-meta.js and the pokeapi csvs, read 2026-11-12/13.

0. DIRECTION
------------
  The gacha is removed. Luck must not hand out an end-game Pokemon and make starters useless; the game
  is a horde ARPG with Pokemon investment and meta progression. Getting a Pokemon is earned in runs,
  and getting a stronger one is earned by evolving it.

1. THE ROUTES
-------------
  route          what it gives                          how
  starter pick   a region's 3 starters                  once, on a new save (section 9)
  rescue         a Stage 1 of the run's region          UberElite fight, then a catch (sections 2 to 4)
  Lost Pokemon   a Stage 1 of any region, with an evo   escort it, then a recruit chance (section 5)
  evolution      everything past Stage 1                candy + the species' requirements (section 6)
  legendary      a region's legendary                   Loop 6 + Legendary Boss (GDD 12, unchanged)

  Stage 1 = a species with no pre-evolution in pokeapi (evolves_from empty). BABIES COUNT: Pichu,
  Cleffa, Munchlax, Riolu are Stage 1; Pikachu, Clefairy, Snorlax, Lucario come only by evolving.
  Exception list (counts as Stage 1 because its pre-evolution is not in the game): Gholdengo.
  Drop Gholdengo from the list if Gimmighoul ever gets SpriteCollab sprites (then: Gimmighoul +
  candy + Gimmighoul Coins from the treasure imp -> Gholdengo).

  NO ROUTE (left out for now): the 4 Paradoxes (Iron Leaves, Iron Valiant, Roaring Moon, Walking Wake;
  BST 590, rarity 5), the 21 mythicals (Arceus stays the endgame superboss, never catchable),
  Perrserker and Clodsire (need regional forms), and every species with no sprites (Shelmet, Gimmighoul).

2. RESCUE EVENT (in run)
------------------------
  CADENCE
    every minute of run time the game rolls for a rescue: 4% base, +4% after each failed roll,
    back to 4% after a rescue fires. About 1.4 / 3.1 / 4.7 / 9.8 rescues at 10 / 20 / 30 / 60 min;
    7.7% of runs see none in the first 10 min; a rescue is certain by roll 25.
    The roll keeps going during everything (gyms, elites, other events). No cap per run.
    It runs on the run clock, so it stops while the run clock stops (pause menu, catch phase).
    It also fires when the belt is empty.

  THE EVENT
    toast + arrow (like shops and gyms), naming the nature:  "A Timid Dratini needs help!"
    stays up 60 s; not reached in time = gone ("miss it, it's gone")
    at the spot: an UBERELITE guards it (above an elite, near a strong gym leader Pokemon)
      time limit X to defeat it: [Open] set in playtest, start at 60 s
      defeated in time ......... catch phase (section 3), and its time left is locked in
      too slow ................. the rescue mon runs off, no catch
      belt empty when it falls . the UberElite's drops only, no catch

  WHICH SPECIES
    pool = Stage 1s of the run region's OWN GEN only (Kanto = Gen 1, Johto = Gen 2 ...), wild
           (rarity 1 to 4), not legendary or mythical, has sprites, + the exception list.
           Starters stay in the pool (a rescue can give a second copy).
           Never-evolving strong Stage 1s are in (Snorlax, Lapras, Aerodactyl, the Ultra Beasts);
           the catch equation (section 3) is their gate.
    pick   weighted by star: 1 star x8, 2 star x4, 3 star x2, 4 star x1.
    the nature is rolled when the rescue is created, shown on the toast, and kept by the caught copy.

    region   pool   1 star 2 star 3 star 4 star   share of rescues (1/2/3/4 star %)
    Kanto     63      32     23      4      4      71 26  2  1
    Johto     48      24     12     10      2      73 18  8  1
    Hoenn     63      37     14     12      0      79 15  6  0
    Sinnoh    38      20     14      4      0      71 25  4  0
    Unova     62      36     15     10      1      78 16  5  0
    Kalos     31      14     12      3      2      67 29  4  1
    Alola     43      13      8     12     10      61 19 14  6
    Galar     34      23      0      6      5      92  0  6  2
    Paldea    42      24      3      3     12      86  5  3  5   (+ Gholdengo)

3. CATCH PHASE
--------------
    UberElite down in time ── time left locked in: t = time left / X (0 to 1)
         │
         ▼  the GAME PAUSES (no horde, no damage; run clock, rescue roll and timed effects stop)
    belt strip: [P][P][P][G][G][U]
         pick a ball: click it in the strip, or press its number key (1 Poke, 2 Great, 3 Ultra)
         the BEST stocked ball starts selected (Ultra > Great > Poke)
         throw ── caught ──> new unique copy in the Pokebox, game resumes
                └ miss ───> Flee meter +1 ── full ──> it runs off, game resumes
         belt empty ──> it runs off, game resumes

  CATCH ODDS (E3, the stepped ladder)
    P = min(90%, base x ball x (1 + t))         ball: Poke 1 | Great 1.5 | Ultra 2
    base slides linearly by BST inside each star band, with a drop between bands
    (for Stage 1s the stars are exact BST bands):
      star   BST band    base at band bottom -> top
       1     175-319         80% -> 60%
       2     320-405         55% -> 40%
       3     410-490         35% -> 22%
       4     495-570         18% ->  6%
    [Open] the 90% ceiling (playtest)

  FLEE METER (by the rescue mon's nature)
    bucket    misses allowed   natures
    easy           5           Bold, Calm, Lax, Relaxed, Docile, Gentle, Mild, Quiet
    default        3           Hardy, Lonely, Brave, Adamant, Impish, Serious, Modest, Bashful,
                               Sassy, Quirky
    hard           1           Timid, Careful, Hasty, Jolly, Naive, Rash, Naughty
    The hard bucket holds 4 of the 5 +Speed natures on purpose: a nature moves movement speed
    (G.Stats.speedToPx takes it), a big early edge in a hordelike, so they are the prize catches.
    [Open] bucket values 5 / 3 / 1 (playtest)

  WHAT IT ADDS UP TO (6-ball belt, 50% time left, random nature)
    1 star ~97% | 2 star ~92% (Poke) | 3 star ~72% (Poke), ~94% (Ultra) | 4 star ~39% (Poke), ~61% (Ultra)
    For 1 and 2 star mons the UberElite fight is the real gate; ball quality matters from 3 star up.

  CATCHES ARE HELD UNTIL THE RUN ENDS (Jess, 2026-11-13; replaces "temp slot only when the box is full")
    Every catch (rescue) and recruit (Lost Pokemon) of a run is HELD, not placed in the Pokebox mid-run.
    No limit on how many are held. When the run ends:
      run ends by:                         held Pokemon
      FALL (the team faints) ............. KEPT
      CRASH (crash save) ................. KEPT
      QUIT (End Run, sign out) ........... ALL LOST (as a quit loses EVs)
    Kept ones go to the Pokebox at the main menu:
      room for all ──> placed, a short "joined your Pokebox" notice
      not enough room ──> ONE LIST of the held Pokemon: keep (needs a free place) or sell;
                          whatever is not placed is SOLD at the copy's sell price
    Shedinja is not a catch: it is made in the Team Menu, and a full box blocks that evolution (section 6).
    The run's crash save stores the held Pokemon (a new crash-save field, defaults empty).

    [Open] rule details to approve when built:
      FB4 other run endings: Return Home and Next Region after a loop (BOSS-REWORK 4a), a Legendary
          Boss win or loss: KEPT (the run ended normally) | other
      FB5 End Run is a quit, so ending a run by choice loses every catch of that run (the #103 End Run
          screen already gives no EVs on a quit). Accept | End Run keeps catches, only sign-out loses them
      FB6 show the held Pokemon during the run (pause menu Run Info, or a counter on the HUD) | hidden

4. BELT AND BALLS
-----------------
  belt     ONE SHARED COUNT of balls, any mix of tiers. A new account starts with 6.
  balls    used up per throw; restocked between runs with coins, any mix up to belt size.
  tiers    Poke (from the start) | Great 1.5x | Ultra 2x. Great and Ultra each unlock with a one-time
           Shop purchase, then restock like Poke Balls.
  meta     the progression dials are belt size (adds slots) and ball quality (the unlocks).
  [Open]   prices: balls by tier, belt size steps, the two unlocks (economy pass)

5. LOST POKEMON (in run, existing event)
----------------------------------------
  The existing events.js event (picked with horde / gold every 240 to 300 s, never twice in a row;
  120 s to deliver) becomes the second recruit route.
  pool     Stage 1s THAT HAVE AN EVOLUTION, rarity 1 to 3, not legendary, from ANY region (not the run's
           region, on purpose), floating species included (the hover filter is dropped): 309 species.
  toast    names the nature: "A Timid Eevee is lost!" (the nature has no flee role here)
  delivery heal 50% HP + coins (the old ticket prize becomes coins) + a recruit chance by star:
           1 star 20% | 2 star 12% | 3 star 6%. A recruit is a new unique copy with the shown nature.
  [Open]   build detail: floaters escort with ground collision or hover movement; spawn and mark points
           for floaters (H1, H2 in v1).

6. EVOLUTION (the only way past Stage 1)
----------------------------------------
  CANDY
    Exp Candy is an EVOLUTION METER ONLY: no stats, every run still starts at level 1. Permanent
    power comes from the evolved form's base stats.
    sizes   XS 100 | S 800 | M 3,000 | L 10,000 | XL 30,000 XP
    cost    the mainline XP curve: the species' growth rate, to its evolution level.
            Abra > Kadabra 2,535 | Bulbasaur > Venusaur 27,021 | Charmander > Charizard 40,007 |
            median line 33,084 | Dratini > Dragonite 207,968 | Deino > Hydreigon 327,680
    source  gym leaders drop it.
    [Parked] drop table, loop scaling, other sources, overflow past the last evolution (CD1 to CD4)

  WHERE AND HOW
    Evolving happens in the TEAM MENU, when the copy's candy level is reached and every requirement is met.
    In-run requirements are TRUE / FALSE FLAGS saved on the copy, set during a run. Back at the main menu
    a popup says the mon is ready ("Runerigus is ready to evolve!").
    Evolutions added in a later gen are always allowed, in any region (Golbat > Crobat in Kanto).
    Gym rosters are unaffected: they keep ignoring evolution requirements (line-completion rule unchanged).

  REQUIREMENTS (each on top of candy; mainline requirement, adapted)
    requirement            n     rule in this game
    plain level          297     candy only
    stone                 50     classic stones DROP FROM GYMS
    trade                 26     a LINK CABLE (one-time use, Shop) or a real trade once multiplayer
                                 exists (deferred)
      trade + held item   17     Link Cable AND the held item (Shop), e.g. Onix > Steelix = Cable + Metal Coat
    friendship            18     N minutes on the TEAM in one run (being on the team is enough);
                                 [Open] N per species (pokeapi base_happiness is one possible dial)
    time of day           18     flag: it was the ACTIVE Pokemon while it was day (or night); needs the
                                 day / night cycle (#101, parked, run time, random start point)
    knows a move          14     flag: the move was in that copy's RUN MOVESET
                                 8 moves are not in the game yet and get imported: Mimic, Double Hit,
                                 Rollout, Taunt, Barb Barrage, Twin Beam, Hyper Drill, Dragon Cheer
    gender, nature,               the copy's own data (copies.js already rolls gender)
    Atk vs Def
    hold item + level      3     the item (Shop): Oval Stone, Razor Claw, Razor Fang
    party member           2     flag: ran with a Remoraid (Mantine) / a Dark type (Pangoro)
    random at evolve       4     rolled when it evolves (Wurmple > Silcoon / Cascoon ...)
    in-run feats           6     flag (#91-1 run counters): take damage, recoil, use a move N times,
                                 walk N steps, level up in battle
    newer use-items        6     SHOP: Black Augurite, Tart / Sweet / Syrupy Apple,
                                 Auspicious / Malicious Armor

  BRANCHING (19 split points)
    The requirements decide the branch; using an item counts as choosing that branch.
    When two or more branches qualify at once (Eevee, Yamask), the player picks among them.

  ONE-OFFS
    Shedinja   evolving Nincada makes Ninjask AND a Shedinja copy. The Shedinja uses up a Poke Ball from
               stock and copies Nincada's IVs, nature and EVs. A full Pokebox or no ball in stock BLOCKS
               the evolution until fixed.
    Kingambit  flag: one Bisharp elite is defeated while Bisharp is on the team. In an Unova-or-later
               run with Bisharp on the team, the run's FIRST elite is a guaranteed Bisharp, using
               Bisharp's own band (T4). Kanto to Sinnoh runs never field it.
    Alcremie   Milcery + Strawberry Sweet (Shop) + flag: the active mon circles for N s. [Open] N (playtest)
    Accelgor   Karrablast + Link Cable + Shed Shell (Shop). Karrablast + Link Cable alone = Escavalier.
    Gholdengo  a catchable Stage 1 (section 1 exception list).

7. WONDER GUARD (Shedinja, new combat rule)
--------------------------------------------
  Shedinja has a FIXED 1 HP (a per-species exception in the G.Stats HP formula).
    typed hit, super-effective ........... lands (1 HP = faint)
    typed hit, not super-effective ....... 0 damage, its side effects still roll
    untyped hit .......................... 0 damage, side effects still roll
    type-immune hit (Normal, Fighting) ... 0 damage, NO side effects (slice 4 immunity rule, unchanged)
    status move .......................... lands
    burn, poison, confusion, weather,
    recoil, hazards, traps, other DoT .... goes through
    gym leaders .......................... same rule (Lt. Surge and Erika have no move that can hit it;
                                           accepted)
  order: type chart first (x0 ends the hit), then Wonder Guard (damage to 0, side effects on).
  HP upgrades on Shedinja (HP cards, HP IVs and EVs, heals) show GREYED OUT, "no effect".
  A revive brings it back at 1 HP. The game's sandstorm and snow damage hit Bug / Ghost, so weather
  faints it (mainline-accurate). Side effects rolling on blocked hits departs from mainline (Jess).

8. GACHA REMOVAL
----------------
  Removed entirely: banners, pulls, pity, the Shop's Pokemon tab content (gacha.js, gacha-ui.js).
  Tickets (x1 shown as "Pokeball", x10 as "Master Ball") are retired. Everything that paid tickets
  pays COINS instead: wild kills (pickup.js TICKET_RATE), locked chests, the Lost Pokemon prize,
  the Gholdengo treasure imp, daily and weekly missions, achievements. Ticket counts leave the HUD,
  icons and summary.
  The rarity field (1 to 5) stays: it is the star tier, the spawn rule (< 5) and the sell price.
  [Parked] shinies: the gacha was the main source; decided with the GDD 12 endgame shiny tiers.

9. NEW SAVE
-----------
  The rework ships with a NEW SAVE, no migration: a one-off owner exception to CLAUDE.md "Save-data
  migration safety" (precedent: docs/decisions/2026-11-03-pokebox-is-a-clean-save-checkpoint.md).
    load ── no save ─────────────────────────────> new save
         ── save older than the acquisition version ──> copied to a backup key (once)
                                                        a notice on the menu
                                                        new save
         ── current save ─────────────────────────> loads as normal
  Detection is a save version bump (js/core/db.js has VERSION and the K_PREBOX backup pattern).
  Nothing carries over (no copies, tickets, coins, Pokedex seen list or settings).
  A new save: pick a REGION (all 9 offered, not tied to which regions are open), get its 3 starters
  (grass, fire, water), plus a coin amount set in the economy pass.
  Note: Kanto has 5 species flagged starter (Psyduck and Slowpoke are test flags); the trio is the
  grass / fire / water three.
