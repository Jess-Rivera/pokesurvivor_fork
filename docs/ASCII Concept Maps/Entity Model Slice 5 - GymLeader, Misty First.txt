WHY
  Today a leader's mon is a wild body with a brain bolted on. Slices 1 to 4 built the parts so it can be
  a Player entity instead: same HP formula, same hit defense, same status engine as you, driven by AI.
  Slice 5 does this for Misty only, behind a per-gym switch. Every other gym stays on the old body.
  One mon, all moves in one group, as today. Teams are slice 7, one active slot is slice 8.

  Key choice: ONE brain, not two. The GymBrain stays the single decision maker and talks to whichever
  body it has. So "the old brain" never forks, and a brain fix reaches every gym. Only the BODY is
  duplicated until slice 12, which narrows the accepted duplication in the plan.

  Each frame, Misty: the brain decides, the controller hands PlayerEntity.update a heading, and the base
  class moves her by your rules. Frozen or asleep, the brain does not step (as today: no recharge either).

  What the leader takes from where
    from PlayerEntity    stats, statuses, stages, walls, hits, payHp, heal, STAB, status pills
    from the GymBrain    every decision, tells, cast timing
    its own              the attack factor (today's numbers, slice 6 changes it), the enrage trigger,
                         faint effects and drops, corpse fade, boss glow and gold bar, AI shake off

 WHAT THE CODE SCAN FOUND (the plan's outline did not list these)
=====================================================================================

  A. SIX WAYS YOUR HITS WOULD COME OUT WRONG. A hit on a Player entity takes the path built for leader
     hits on you, which never needed these:
       gap                                     without a fix, on Misty
       --------------------------------------  ------------------------------------------
       Expert Belt, Charcoal-type held items   silently stop working
       Power Stone (resist floor and pierce)   stops working on a resisted or immune type
       Fixed and level damage (Seismic Toss)   does NOTHING, a console note only
       Damage dealt, Shell Bell                stop counting
       Status-category damage moves            use her Def instead of the neutral 50
       Melee cushion (25% less)                ALWAYS ON, since Tackle sits in her one group (D4)
     All closed in step A, with the parity harness proving each.

  B. FLAT DAMAGE CALLS WOULD MISFIRE. The two families' hurt takes different arguments, so a bomb passes
     its COLOR where a Player entity expects a TYPE. Sites: bomb (game.js), ground traps (interact.js),
     flat moves and Fissure (move-effects.js), Rocky Helmet (combat.js). See D1.

  C. MISSING METHODS WOULD CRASH. Ground traps and G.Debug call applyPoison, applySlow, applyBurn and
     applyStatus on whatever is in the enemy list. The leader answers all four through G.Status.

  D. HUMAN-ONLY THINGS IN THE BASE hurt(). The dev log writes every hit as a hit on YOU, so a leader
     would corrupt the log. Faint has no hook for drops, fanfare or the Snowball Badge. The enemy
     manager needs an id and a fade to hold a corpse. Each gets a small hook or gate.

  E. ALREADY TRUE, NO WORK. Hit i-frames off (a wild body never had any); knockback (bosses already
     ignore it); type chart and weather (both paths apply them); defense math (same formula at DMG_SCALAR,
     level and stage, confirmed in stats.js); liquid (all of Misty's roster is Water, so unslowed. Lava
     for other gyms is checked at slice 12).

  F. THE SOUND QUESTION (STATE "decide which select, shot and heal sounds are the human's").
     None of the three fire in slice 5: a leader never calls setActive, cast or revive, and leader casts
     are already skipped by move:cast. No sound changes now. It returns at slice 8 with slot switching;
     the recommendation then is human only, since they announce YOUR actions.

 DECISIONS FOR JESS
=====================================================================================

  D1. Flat damage. RECOMMEND A.
      A: one new "take flat damage" entry that both families answer, and all flat sites call it. Clean,
         and it extends slice 4's contract. About 7 call sites, proven by the existing harnesses.
      B: the leader's hurt guesses which argument shape it got. Smaller, but fragile, and against the
         structure-first rule.

  D2. Misty's hpMult = 26 on loop 1, carried forward to later loops the same way sets and cards carry.
      RECOMMEND yes. A loop number key allows a per-mon override later, as the plan says.

  D3. First restrictions block. The plan text says tells, heal limit and contact. RECOMMEND tells (on or
      off) and heal limit only, with today's values as the defaults. Contact is left out until a gym
      really wants it back, since building it for a Player entity costs real code that nothing would use.
      Your plan rule is "grows only when a slice needs an entry". Tell lengths also wait.

  D4. Melee cushion (25% less damage while a melee move is active). RECOMMEND off for leaders until slice 8,
      then decide. With all moves in one group it would be permanently on, a hidden +33% to her bulk.

  D5. Flinch. RECOMMEND today's leader flinch (cancels the tell) until slice 8. Your rule restarts the
      active group's cooldowns, and with one group that would restart ALL her moves on every flinch.

  D6. Her look. RECOMMEND:
      - Hit numbers: today's (type color, yellow super, orange crit, always big), not your red ones.
      - Body: the Player-entity draw, plus today's boss glow and gold bar. NEW, and it should read as
        better: her status pills and her stage arcs now show, the same as yours.

 AS BUILT (2026-11-06), where it differs from the plan above
=====================================================================================
  - One working tree, not four commits: steps A to C landed in the same files in one session.
  - Parity runs at runtime instead of as a pin written first: gym-leader-harness hits the old body and the
    leader with the same hit, both against the hand number, since the old body still exists.
  - The leader status rule is a cut of 0.5 on freeze and sleep, not separate numbers: 2 s becomes 1 s and
    2 to 6 s becomes 1 to 3 s, so a move's own stated length scales too. Same approved table.
  - Rocky Helmet stays on hurt, not the flat entry: it only fires on touch, and a leader has no touch.
  - Fissure's KO stays on hurt: it is guarded against every Player entity before the call.
  - Restrictions live at the gym level only (tells, healLimit). Misty authors none, so the defaults apply.
  - The stuck side-step of the wild body is not carried over; the leader keeps G.Path and wall sliding.
    Watch for her catching on an arena rock in the feel test.
  - The move halo under her body shows her first move's type, since all moves sit in one group until slice 8.
