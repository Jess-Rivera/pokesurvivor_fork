Branch: feature/pokeboxes. Spec: docs/proposals/pokebox-plan.md "Rulings for step 3" (sell price) and "Step 3 handoff".

=====================================================================
 SELL PRICE  [Approved 2026-11-03]
=====================================================================

   rarity      1*   2*   3*   4*    5*
   normal      15   30   60   120   300
   shiny       45   90   180  360   900     (x3)

   Rarity = the species' rarity (same stars the box cell shows).

=====================================================================
 WHERE YOU SELL
=====================================================================

 A) TEAM SCREEN, a copy selected from a box (detail pane, under Moves)

   +---------------------------------------+
   | Pikachu (m)            No 025 · Kanto |
   | ** rarity  [Electric]                 |
   | ( hexagon )                           |
   | Nature  Ability  Height  Weight       |
   | Moves ...                             |
   | [ (c) Sell for 30 ]                   |   <- copy not on the team
   +---------------------------------------+
     on the team (saved, or in your uncommitted edits):
   | [ Sell for 30 ] dimmed                |
   |   "Remove it from your team first."   |

   click -> confirm "Sell Pikachu for 30 PokeCoins?"
            note: "This is your last Pikachu. The Pokedex keeps it."  (only if last)
         -> sold: coins +30, saved at once (like a box move), the box
            cell is gone, the detail pane shows the species (Pokedex view),
            tab counts and top bar update (3a-2b listeners)

   Pokedex tab (a species, no copy chosen): no sell button.

 B) GACHA RESULTS, after a pull (the cards)

   +--------+  +--------+  +--------+
   | NEW!   |  | To Box1|  | To Box1|
   |  mon   |  |  mon   |  |  mon   |
   |        |  |[Sell+30]| |[Sell+60]|
   +--------+  +--------+  +--------+
      new        repeat      repeat

   click Sell on a repeat:
     1* to 3*, not shiny   -> sold at once, no dialog
     4* / 5* or shiny      -> confirm first
   then the card reads "Sold +30" (no undo), coins +30

=====================================================================
 WHAT CAN'T BE SOLD
=====================================================================

   on the saved team ............ no ("Remove it from your team first.")
   in uncommitted team edits .... no (same message)
   locked ........................ no (nothing sets a lock until 3b; the rule
                                       is in now so 3b is only the button)
   already sold / not found ...... no ("That Pokemon is gone.")

=====================================================================
 DECISIONS FOR JESS  (each a rule, approve or change)
=====================================================================

 D1  The last copy of a species CAN be sold, with a "your last one" note.
     The Pokedex keeps the species (owned stays), so collection
     achievements and the Pokedex tab do not drop.           [closes plan Open]
     REC: yes

 D2  A copy in your uncommitted team edits counts as on the team.
     REC: yes (otherwise you could sell a Pokemon you just slotted)

 D3  Gacha cards: only repeats get a Sell button; new cards do not
     (sell those from the box).                              REC: yes, fewer misclicks

 D4  Gacha card confirm: none for 1* to 3* normals, a confirm for 4*, 5*
     and any shiny.                                          REC: yes
     (repeats of commons are the 10-pull clutter; a dialog per card is slow)

 D5  The box always confirms (every rarity).                 REC: yes
     (selling there is deliberate and rare until 3b bulk sell)

 D6  After a box sale the detail pane shows the species, not the next copy.
     REC: yes (selecting a neighbour risks a second accidental sale)
