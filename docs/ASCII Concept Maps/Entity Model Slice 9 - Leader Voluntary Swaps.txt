9b - SWAP DECISIONS  (inside G.GymLeaderAI.swapSlot; the v1 rules, unchanged in substance)
  MAY SHE SWAP?  2+ living | lock 0 | not trapped | not in the air   (Team.canSwap, yours)
                 + no tell running | no move commitment | on field >= MIN_FIELD 8 s | swaps left     (the AI)
  WHY? first that fires
     1 HARD CC   asleep or frozen, >= 1.5 s left                                  --> healthiest bench mon
     2 LOW HP    < 25%, no self heal left, this mon not saved yet                 --> best matchup mon >= 50% HP
     3 MATCHUP   best bench matchup >= 2x the active's                            --> that mon (>= 40% HP)
  MATCHUP  offense(m) = best open damaging move: type x your types x STAB 1.5
           defense(m) = max(0.25, your active move's type x m's types)      score = offense / defense
     Bulbasaur, Vine Whip:  Staryu 1 / 2 = 0.5   Seel 2 / 2 = 1.0   --> 1.0 >= 2 x 0.5, Seel in after 8 s
     Charmander, Ember:     Staryu 3 / 0.5 = 6   Seel 3 / 0.5 = 6   --> no swap
  SWAP EFFECTS  outgoing: shapes and commitments end (G.MoveSim.clear), withdraw (no kill paid), keeps HP,
                statuses, own drops; cooldowns freeze on the bench.  incoming: Team.swap (0.5 s i-frames, 5 s lock),
                enters the list, bossId follows. Ring + "<leader> switches to <mon>!", no camera kick.
  BENCH FAINT   enters the list already fainted with no fade: the normal kill payer pays it once (you get the
                kill, EVs, Silver), silent, no gym drop; a bench wipe in the send-out beat wins, drop at arena center
