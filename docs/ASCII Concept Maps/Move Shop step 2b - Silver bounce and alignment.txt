  So the bounce starts and lands with no visible jump from the spin.

 2. ALIGNMENT FIX (the bug)
=====================================================================

  today:  coin centred on a line, number centred on the same line.
          The pixel font's digits and the coin have different empty
          margins, so their bottoms miss.

  fix:    ONE ground line. The coin's lowest opaque row sits on it, and
          the number's baseline sits on it (digits have no descenders).

     XP bar  ═══════════════════════════  y 0..7
             (o)  ← bounce peak, top at y 12
     
             (o) 1,234
     ground ──────────────  y 42   coin bottom + number bottom

  The ground moves from about y 39 to y 42 so the bounce peak
  clears the XP bar.

 4. DECISIONS FOR JESS
=====================================================================

  E1  THE POP (changes step 2 D1)
      proposed:  drop the 1.3x pop. The bounce is the bump now, and the
                 number keeps rolling up. Two motions at once is busy.
      alt:       keep the pop on the number only, bounce on the coin

  E2  A GAIN DURING A BOUNCE  (one bounce is 1.0 s, kills come faster)
      proposed:  PLAY THROUGH. A bounce is never restarted. If more
                 Silver came in mid-air, the coin hops again as soon as
                 it lands. At high kill rates it keeps hopping, one hop
                 a second; when kills stop it lands and goes back to
                 spinning. The rolling number still shows every gain.
      alt A:     restart on every gain (the D1 rule). At several kills
                 a second the coin never leaves the ground and jitters.
      alt B:     play through, but at double speed (0.5 s a hop)

  E3  SPIN DURING THE HOP
      proposed:  the coin does not spin during a hop (the bounce art
                 has its own turn at frames 1 and 8). If a gain lands
                 mid-spin it snaps to the face-on frame, one 125 ms
                 frame, barely visible.
