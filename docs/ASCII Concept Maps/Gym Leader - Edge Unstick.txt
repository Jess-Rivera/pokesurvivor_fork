GYM LEADER - EDGE UNSTICK   (Jess 2026-11-07, approved: "Smart side")
=====================================================================

PROBLEM
  Misty (G.GymLeader, a Player entity) stutters on the arena edge.
  Walls already slide her (G.World.collide pushes out, keeps the tangent).
  The arena rim is a staircase of square tiles; backing straight away from
  you, her slide catches on each tile corner. The wild body has a stuck
  side-step (enemy.js); slice 5 did not carry it over.

FLOW (one frame, js/entities/gym-leader.js update)
  brain heading (steer) --> side-step active? --yes--> turn heading 1.25 rad (72 deg) to `side`
                                  | no
                                  v
  remember x, y --> Player entity step (move + wall slide, unchanged)
                                  v
  moved < 35% of heading x spd x dt ? --yes, for > 0.35 s--> pick side, side-step 0.7 s
            | no --> stuck clock = 0

  NEVER COUNTS AS STUCK
    no heading (attacking, stunned) | dash (mvRoot) | flinch | push (Roar)
    dodge | Fly | root (trap) | ice slide

PICK SIDE (the "smart side")
  probe both turned headings 24 px ahead, a test circle of her radius,
  sliding on walls in 8 px steps (G.World.collide), measure how far it got
    only one side gets >= 4 px further ----> that side
    both about even (gap < 4 px) ----------> the side she circles (brain.side)
    both get < 4 px (shut) ----------------> random, as wild

NUMBERS  G.GymLeader.UNSTICK
  slip 0.35 | stuck 0.35 s | side 0.7 s | turn 1.25 rad   <- the wild body's
  probe 24 px | tie 4 px | shut 4 px                      <- smart side

SCOPE
  AI only: the human player is untouched (steer/update live in the subclass).
  Run-scoped fields (stuckT, sideT, side): no save data, no load order change.
