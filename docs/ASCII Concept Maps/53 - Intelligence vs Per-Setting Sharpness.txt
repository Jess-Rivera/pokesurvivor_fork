53 - INTELLIGENCE vs PER-SETTING SHARPNESS  (Quick, 2026-11-13, branch feature/gym-leader-ai)
Question (Jess): fold misjudge and the per-loop sharpness growth into one "intelligence" score?
Status: comparison only, nothing decided. Spec today: decision 2026-11-09-49-leader-sharpness-base-plus-loop-growth.

=====================================================================================
THE THREE METHODS
=====================================================================================

  A  PER-SETTING (today's spec)        B  INTELLIGENCE ONLY              C  INTELLIGENCE + OVERRIDES
  ---------------------------          --------------------              -------------------------
  each sharpness setting:              one number per leader:            B, plus a leader may set any
    misjudge   base + growth             intelligence = base + growth      of the four directly; that
    reflex     base + growth                    |                          setting then leaves the
    dodgeRead  base + growth            curves in code turn it into        intelligence curve
    memoryUse  base + growth            misjudge, reflex, dodgeRead,
                                        memoryUse

  numbers per leader:   8              2                                 2 (+ the rare override)
  for 12 leaders:       96             24                                24 + a handful

=====================================================================================
PROS AND CONS
=====================================================================================

  A  PER-SETTING
     +  the most control: a leader can be sharp one way and dull another
     +  already decided (2026-11-09); no curves to design
     +  what you set is exactly what runs (no mapping in between)
     -  96 numbers to tune by feel across 12 leaders x 6 loops
     -  no single number says "how hard is this leader": comparing Brock loop 6 to Lance loop 1 means
        reading four settings each
     -  easy to author an incoherent leader (instant reflexes, terrible plans) without meaning to
     -  retuning a loop's difficulty touches four numbers per leader

  B  INTELLIGENCE ONLY
     +  2 numbers a leader; one blunt dial, the genre's proven pattern (Brotato Danger, Halls Agony)
     +  difficulty reads at a glance and compares across leaders and loops
     +  leaders stay coherent: every sharpness setting rises together
     +  the harness tests one curve family instead of 96 values
     -  every leader is smart in the same SHAPE; style settings (aggression, range, risk) still differ
     -  can't say "Misty reads you fast but plans no better" (the #39 brief asks for exactly that)
     -  the curves become load-bearing: one bad curve skews all 12 leaders at once
     -  new work: 4 curves to design and tune (TUNE in code)
     -  "this leader feels off" no longer points straight at one setting

  C  INTELLIGENCE + OVERRIDES
     +  B's simplicity by default; 2 numbers for most leaders
     +  the escape hatch for real exceptions (Misty's reflex, a Koga that misjudges on purpose)
     +  overrides reuse today's authoring (a number, or keyed by loop), so no new shape for them
     +  intelligence still compares leaders; an override is visible in the leader's data
     -  two ways to set one thing: needs a clear rule (an override wins and drops that setting's growth)
     -  override creep: a leader overriding all four is just A again, harder to read
     -  the validator and the overlay must say where each value came from
     -  same curve work as B

=====================================================================================
SIDE BY SIDE
=====================================================================================

                          A per-setting   B intelligence   C intel + overrides
  tuning load             high (96)       low (24)         low (24 + few)
  expressiveness          full            low              full where needed
  difficulty at a glance  no              yes              yes
  coherent leaders        by care         built in         built in, opt-out visible
  new code                none            4 curves         4 curves + precedence rule
  fits #39 brief          yes             partly           yes
  risk                    tuning sprawl   curve skews all  override creep

=====================================================================================
VERDICT: C is the stronger option
=====================================================================================

  - 12 leaders x 6 loops tuned by feel: the dial has to be one number, or tuning sprawls (A's weakness).
  - #39 already asks for a leader that is sharp one way ("Misty reads you fast"): B can't say it, C can.
  - the override costs little: it reuses the per-loop authoring that exists today.
  - guard against creep: the validator warns when a leader overrides 3 or more of the four
    (then it should ask whether intelligence is the right base for that leader).
