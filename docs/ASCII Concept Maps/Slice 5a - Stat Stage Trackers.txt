2. ONE TRACKER = (Pokemon, move, stat)        Gate 6.0 s   Duration 6.1 s
========================================

  success --> tracker exists? --no--> apply stages, start Gate + Duration
                   |
                  yes --> reset Duration (always)
                   |
                   +----> Gate open? --yes--> add stages again, restart Gate
                                     --no---> refresh only

  no success for 6.1 s --> this tracker = 0 (other trackers untouched)
  stage on a host = sum of its trackers, clamped to +-6
  each tracker also clamped to +-6 on its own (Jess OK, SS6)

4. WHO ROLLS, WHERE IT LANDS        (js/systems/move-effects.js)
============================
  hit --> stat applier
            |-- foe drop ----> roll per enemy hit -------> tracker on enemy   Gate 6
            |-- self drop ---> roll once per cast, ------> tracker on caster  Gate 12
            |                  on its first enemy hit
            '-- self raise --> roll once per cast, ------> tracker on team    Gate 6
                               on its first enemy hit
  a cast that hits nothing rolls nothing (SS2): a missed Leaf Storm costs no stat
  persistent / rehit move: every tick is a use
  foe tag becomes move@caster: 2 Pokemon with Bubble Beam = 2 trackers


5. PURE BUFFS CAST ON THEIR GATE    (js/systems/move-sim.js)
================================
  Swords Dance, Agility, Harden, Dragon Dance
      no cooldown ---> cast when tracker missing or Gate open,
                       ONLY while its group is the active group
      inactive ------> no casts; its stages ride the Duration out
                       (solo buff = charge, then swap; Jess, SS8)
      swap in -------> casts at once only with no Gate running;
                       Gate running: wait for it, then cast and climb
      fizzle --------> wait one Gate (Jess OK, SS8)
      Recharge card / Reflejos ---> no effect until team cards land
  Aqua Ring, Substitute, damage moves ---> own cooldown, as today
