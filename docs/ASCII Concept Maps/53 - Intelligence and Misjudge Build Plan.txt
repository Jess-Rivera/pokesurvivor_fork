  CURVES: two straight lines per setting, meeting at the default (0.5). TUNE.
     intelligence    0 (atZero)   0.5 (atDefault)   1 (atOne)    never reaches

     aimError        40 px        10 px             4 px         0
  between two points: a straight line (0.25 sits half way between atZero and atDefault).

  FRACTIONAL DODGE READS (Q2): the bag still holds whole reads per 10 threats.
     dodgeRead 6.4 = 6 per bag, plus readCarry += 0.4 at each new bag; at 1 or more that bag reads 7
     and readCarry drops by 1.   bags 1..10:  6 6 7 6 7 6 6 7 6 7  = exactly 64 reads per 100 threats.
     A counter, not dice (spec DETERMINISTIC RATES). readCarry starts at 0 each fight.

=====================================================================================
MISJUDGE IN THE PLANNER (every think tick)
=====================================================================================

  tree walk (every plan of <= 4 actions)
     each plan path met   --> one seeded noiseDraw in [-1, 1], reused for every goal that plan is scored for
     its cost             --> misreadCost = cost x (1 + misjudge x noiseDraw)            <- D1
     per goal: best plan by (value - misreadCost)  --> the ranking  --> pick (randomness as today)

  so it can pick the wrong plan for a goal AND the wrong goal: "thinks a bad plan is good".
  own seeded source per fight (misjudgeSource), separate from the dodge bag, the prediction bag and
  randomness; the harness fixes it through G.LeaderAI.seed, as the others.
  overlay (G.Debug.hitbox): a new line with chosenPlanNoise (spec OVERLAY).

=====================================================================================

  D1  how the noise misreads a cost
      (a) x (1 + misjudge x noiseDraw): misjudge 0.1 = each plan's cost misread by up to 10%  CHOSEN
          - a plan that trades about even (cost near 0) is barely misread
      (b) + misjudge x 0.1 x noiseDraw, in the HP currency: 0.1 = up to +-0.01 of a max HP per plan
          - misreads every plan the same; the 0.1 scale is one more TUNE number
  D2  growth range: 0 to 0.2 per loop (5 steps reach +1 at most; no negative growth)     CHOSEN
      or allow negative growth (a leader who gets worse each loop)
