=====================================================================
 CLICK FLOW ON A PRICE TAB
=====================================================================

  click (c) price tab
        |
        +-- not the next box? ----> toast "Buy Box N first"      (error sfx)
        |
        +-- not enough coins? ----> toast "Not enough PokeCoins" (error sfx)
        |
        +-- confirm dialog (G.UI.ask)
              title: "Buy box"
              text:  "Spend 10,000 PokeCoins on Box 3? You have 12,000."
              [Buy]  [Cancel]
                |
                +-- Buy: coins -10,000, boxCount 2 -> 3, saves AT ONCE
                         (storage, like dragging a copy to a box tab;
                          pending team edits stay pending until Commit)
                         new Box 3 tab opens, grid redraws

  Dragging a copy onto a price tab: nothing happens, no drop highlight.
