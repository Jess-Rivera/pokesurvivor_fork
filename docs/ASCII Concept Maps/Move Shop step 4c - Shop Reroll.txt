 2. HOW A SHOP REROLL WORKS
=====================================================================

  ┌─ MOVE TRAINER ──────────────────────────────── (o) 4,340 ──┐
  │ Pikachu: [1] Thunderbolt [2] Swift [3] Agility [4] Slam    │
  │                                   [R] Reroll (1 left)       │
  └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
        R or click ──► Trainer: Pikachu's 4 offers re-roll
                       Mart: the 4 TMs re-roll (shared by the team)
                       count - 1, no dialog (free)
        0 left ──► button greyed out "No rerolls left"

  new offers: from the same pool, minus moves it knows, and minus the
              4 just shown while the pool has enough others (so a
              reroll always shows something new when it can)
  count:      per BUILDING, shared by the whole team (Trainer and Mart
              each have their own); the next pair starts fresh
              per building = 1 (baseline, R2) + Shop Rerolls rank (0
              until upgrade points are wired)

                 screen's 1 free reroll per run), so rerolling exists

                 re-rolls the Pokemon you are shopping for.

  R4  COST
      proposed:  free, limited by the count (VS Reroll; matches the
                 level-up reroll). No dialog.
      alt:       Silver per reroll, Brotato-style rising price (would
                 get the confirm dialog)

  R5  OLD BANS STOP APPLYING   (CHANGES EXISTING BEHAVIOR, old saves)
      Saves that set bans before 11-02 still filter level-up cards
      and the Starting Moves extra pick.
