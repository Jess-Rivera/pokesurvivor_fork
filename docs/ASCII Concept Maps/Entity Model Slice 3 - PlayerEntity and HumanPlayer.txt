WHY
  Slice 5 makes a gym leader a Player entity: same moves, slots, Team, dodge and status rules as you,
  but driven by AI. Today one class does both jobs (the rules AND your keyboard, camera, XP, coins).
  Slice 3 splits it so a leader can take the rules without the human parts.

=====================================================================================
 DECISIONS FOR JESS
=====================================================================================
  D1. One file or two.
      Proposed: both classes in js/entities/player.js (base first). A separate
      player-entity.js would mean editing the load list of ~25 harnesses and tools, which the
      plan forbids for a neutral slice. Slice 1 split elite.js because Elite was small and new.

  D2. What isPlayer means.
      Proposed: keep the name, defined as "follows Player entity rules", set on the base. All 13
      sites are already that. No isHuman flag until a site needs one. Renaming would touch the
      fakes in 6 harnesses (they set isPlayer: true by hand).

  D3. Berry guards on a leader. RULE INTERPRETATION, approved table says leaders get none.
      Proposed: the 3 berry sites stay Player-entity rules. A leader never triggers them because
      its berry bag (a Team store) stays empty: leaders pick up no ground items. If a gym ever
      authors berries for a leader, they WOULD work, and we revisit then.
      Alternative: make the 3 sites human-only at slice 5 (strict table reading).

  D4. DECIDED (Jess, 2026-11-06): B. The in-play slot keys (attack 1 to 4, cycle) go through the
      controller now, same call site. setActive is not split (slice 8). Cycle is human-only.

  Left for slice 5 (not decided here): the select/shot/heal sounds stay in the base for now.
  When a leader becomes a Player entity, decide which are the human's.

FEEL TEST (about a minute): one normal run. Walk, dodge, swap, Shake off while confused or
asleep, take hits (camera kick and hurt sound), pick up XP and coins. Nothing should change.
