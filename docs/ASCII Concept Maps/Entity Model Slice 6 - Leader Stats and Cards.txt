GOVERNING RULE (Jess, 2026-11-07; COMBAT-RULES "Host model", LEADER PRINCIPLE): a leader is treated as just
another player's team, controlled by AI. Anything a player can have, a leader can have. Every difference
from a player is hand authored per gym. The D2 recommendation below broke this rule (it followed the stale
SS10 line, now fixed).

WHY
  Slice 5 gave Misty your body: your HP formula, your defense, your statuses. Her ATTACK and her STATS
  are still the wild ones bolted on by the brain. Slice 6 finishes the match: a leader built from the same
  inputs as a player has the same numbers as that player, then times the gym's own knobs (hpMult, power,
  enrage). Misty gets the Zinc x3 that was held back on 11-05, and only Misty is re-tuned.

=====================================================================================
 WHAT THE CODE SCAN FOUND
=====================================================================================

  A. HER ATTACK IS STILL THE WILD FORMULA. The brain replaces her moveFactor with its own
     (boss-ai.js factor: the enemy attack factor x power x enrage x stage). At today's inputs the
     two formulas give the SAME number (neutral IVs, no nature, no Protein), so swapping is neutral.
     What the wild one cannot do: Protein / Calcium, IVs, nature, held items. See D1.

  B. HER DEFENSE ALREADY READS VITAMINS. She is a Player entity, so Iron and Zinc go through the same
     incOf her hurt() already calls, and the brain already gives her a card store. Zinc is blocked
     only by the validator ("leaders use the base stage timers"). Authoring it is one line.

  C. AN ORDER BUG WAITING. Her max HP is set BEFORE the brain loads her cards, so a Vitamins HP Up
     card would be silently ignored. Fixed here even though Misty authors none.

  D. IVs, EVs AND NATURE ALREADY HAVE A HOME. A Player entity takes a stat profile (the same one your
     Pokebox copy builds). A leader passes none today, so she plays neutral.

  E. HELD ITEMS ARE NOT READY. Equipping goes through each item's own on / off code, written for your
     mons. Nobody has checked those for a holder that is not on your team. See D4.

=====================================================================================
 DECISIONS FOR JESS
=====================================================================================

  D1. Her attack uses YOUR attack formula, times power and enrage. RECOMMEND yes. Same numbers today;
      it is what makes IVs, nature and Protein count for a leader.

  D2. Which team cards a leader may hold. RECOMMEND Vitamins only (all six stats). Gate, Duration and
      Regen stay banned: Gate and Duration would change the base stage timers SS10 gives enemies, and
      Regen is a bench heal, which means nothing until she has a bench (slice 7). Authored as
      "Vitamins, Sp. Def, 3 stacks". This rewrites the "no team cards" line in COMBAT-RULES SS10 and
      BOSS-REWORK section 3 to "Vitamins only".

  D3. IVs, EVs and nature. RECOMMEND build the authoring keys now (per mon, per loop, carried forward
      like move sets; the validator checks IVs 0 to 31, EVs 0 to 252 with 510 total, a real nature),
      but Misty authors NONE this slice, so Zinc is the only stat change you feel.

  D4. Held items. RECOMMEND not in this slice. The first gym that wants one gets the item check then.
      A TODO line records it.

  D5. The fight-time reference. RECOMMEND half physical, half special. The old reference is all special,
      so it would overstate what Zinc does against a physical team (74 s against 70 s at level 16).
      Target 60 s at the usual level; the report prints levels 16 to 24 and FAILS if the usual level
      is more than 10% off target.

  D6. Misty's hpMult 26 -> 25 (TUNE). With Zinc and the 50/50 reference that is about 60 s at level 20,
      67 s at 16, 52 s at 24. Brock and gyms 3 to 8 do not move.

=====================================================================================
 QUESTIONS (open, 2026-11-07)
=====================================================================================
  Q1  Enrage. Today it is the one clock that stops a fight from stalling (x bonus after 120 s). Remove it
      outright for switched gyms, or turn it into an authored restriction (off unless a gym sets it)?
      RECOMMEND the authored restriction, Misty off. The plan's "enrage restarts on each send-out"
      (decision 3) then applies only to gyms that author it.
  Q2  The gym's power and tempo knobs. power multiplies her damage; tempo shortens her cooldowns and her
      tells. Neither exists for a player. RECOMMEND: power goes for switched gyms (cards do that job);
      tempo becomes an authored restriction (default 1), since it is the AI's pace. Misty is at 1 and 1,
      so nothing she does changes.
  Q3  The built-in leader differences from slice 5: the fixed status table (shorter freeze and sleep, the
      5 s window, auto shake), and no hit i-frames. Under the rule these are hidden differences.
      RECOMMEND: the player's rules become the default, and each becomes an authored restriction. Misty
      authors today's values, so her fight does not change. In this slice, or its own slice?
  Q4  Items, both tiers? Held items (one per mon) fit now. Most run items read the TEAM (Brass Knuckles
      and Sharp Scissors need 3 mons, Pikachu's Tear counts faints, Re-Do revives the team), and a
      leader has one mon until slice 7. Items that only serve the human (coins, XP, pickups) would do
      nothing. RECOMMEND: held items now; run items now too but team-reading ones wait for slice 7;
      the validator rejects items that cannot work on a leader.
  Q5  A card budget? A player gets about one card per level. RECOMMEND no hard cap, a validator warning
      when a gym authors more cards than a player of that level could hold.

=====================================================================================
 BALANCE AND RISK
=====================================================================================
  Harder: Zinc cuts special damage on her by about 11% at level 20. Easier: 4% less HP. Net against a special
    team: a little longer; against a physical team: a little shorter. About 60 s either way at level 20.
  Risks: the attack swap (Step 0 pins it, so any drift fails before Step B); a gym harness that pins
    the leader's factor through the brain (it gets a fixture edit, reported with its proof).
  Way out: drop the Zinc line and set hpMult back to 26.

  Feel test (about 2 minutes): G.Debug.gym(2) with a special-heavy team, then a physical one. Special
  hits should feel a touch weaker on her, physical about the same. Fight length about a minute.
