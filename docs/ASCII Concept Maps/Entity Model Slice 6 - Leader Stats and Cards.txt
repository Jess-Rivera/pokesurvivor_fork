 NEW DECISIONS (N1 to N5), flagged as rule changes
=====================================================================================
  N1  Wild-body gyms (Brock, gyms 3 to 8) keep "no team cards" until they switch (slice 12): their body has no
      Vitamins path, so a team card would do nothing. RECOMMEND yes. Only playerEntity gyms get D2.
  N2  D2 side effect: Gate and Duration cards are legal on a leader and change ITS stage timers exactly as they
      change yours. Regen is rejected by the validator until slice 7 (it heals the bench; she has none).
      RECOMMEND yes. Misty authors neither, so nothing she does changes.
  N3  Auto shake default. Your default is "you tap"; an AI has no hands, so "off" would leave a leader frozen
      the full length, a hidden handicap. RECOMMEND default ON at the auto rate (same as a player with the
      accessibility setting on). Misty authors on (today's behavior).
  N4  Tempo stays authored PER SLOT (where it lives now, and per mon fits teams in slice 7), reclassified as the
      AI's pace. The validator rejects power other than 1 on a switched gym. RECOMMEND yes.
  N5  Card budget measure: one stack = one level-up pick; a player at level L has had L - 1 picks. The level is
      only known at gate spawn, so the check runs at fight start as a dev log WARNING (never a block). Misty
      (13 stacks + Zinc 3 = 16) warns at level 16 only. RECOMMEND yes; Misty gets NO held item this slice so
      the feel test isolates Zinc, hpMult and enrage.

      WHY: same numbers today at neutral inputs; it is what makes every player input count.

  [2] ORDER FIX AND STAT PROFILE (C, D3)
      js/entities/gym-leader.js  constructor: set the stat profile from the gym, then max HP, then items, then

      js/systems/gym.js          leader def builder: passes this loop's profile and items into def.gym.
      js/data/gyms.js            new slot keys ivs, evs, nature (per loop, carried forward like move sets).
                                 Validator: IVs 0 to 31, EVs 0 to 252 each and 510 total, a real nature.
      WHY: an HP Up card is silently lost today; IVs and nature need a way in.

  [3] CARDS (D2, N1, N2)
      js/data/gyms.js  card validator: team cards legal on playerEntity gyms, still rejected on the wild body;
                       Regen rejected ("waits for slice 7"); team cards skip the "fits a fielded move" check
                       (they target the mon, not a move); caps still apply on the running total.
      WHY: D2. The rest of the card path (team card store, Vitamins read by Def and Sp. Def) already works.

  [4] ITEMS, BOTH TIERS (Q4)
      js/data/gyms.js         slot key `item` (one held item, per loop, carried forward); gym key `runItems`
                              (gained per loop, cumulative, like cards). Validator: the item exists, the right
                              tier in the right key, max 4 run items (your cap), and not on the leader reject
                              list below.
      js/entities/gym-leader.js  equips the held item and puts each run item on her (same on / off code as yours).
      Leader reject list (validator message names the reason):
        human only ......... Amulet Coin (coins), Lucky Egg (XP)
        reads YOUR side .... Snowball Badge (counts your kills, run-wide)
        reads a team ....... Brass Knuckles, Sharp Scissors, Pikachu's Tear, Re-Do  (back in slice 7)
        + any held item step 0 finds whose effect only fires on your code path (named in the step 0 report)
      WHY: the team-HP and faint math reads YOUR roster today, so a leader holding Brass Knuckles would scale
      off your team's HP. That is a real bug, not just a no-op.

  [5] ENRAGE AND POWER (Q1, Q2, N4)
      js/data/gyms.js   new restriction `enrage` (true / false, absent = off). Validator: power must be 1 on a
                        switched gym.
      js/systems/gym.js enrage multiplier and countdown return "none" for a switched gym without `enrage`, so
                        the HUD countdown disappears on its own (hud.js not touched).
      WHY: Q1 and Q2. "Enrage restarts on each send-out" (host-model-plan decision 3) applies only where authored.

  [6] STATUS RULES AS RESTRICTIONS (Q3, N3)
      js/data/gyms.js        new restrictions: statusCut (freeze and sleep length, 0 to 1, default 1),
                             ccWindow (seconds of no new hard CC, default your 2.5), autoShake (default ON, N3),

      js/systems/status.js   the leader status table reads the holder's authored values; a leader with none
                             gets your rules.
      js/entities/gym-leader.js  i-frames and auto shake read the restrictions.
      WHY: Q3, the last hidden differences become authored ones.

  [7] CARD BUDGET (Q5, N5)
      js/systems/gym.js  at fight start, a dev log warning when stacks > level - 1. No block.

  [8] MISTY (step B only)
