=====================================================================
 DECISIONS FOR JESS  (each changes behavior; approve or change)
=====================================================================

 G1  Amount: one berry lowers its stat by 10 EVs, never below 0. It can't
     be used on a stat at 0.                         REC: yes (mainline -10)

 G2  The lowered EVs are lost, not returned to a pool. The copy can earn
     them again (the 252 / 510 room opens up).        REC: yes (mainline;
     the run banks have no carry-over by design, so a refund pool would be
     a second bank)

 G3  Source: the Shop's Items tab, for coins, always available. 300 coins
     each, TUNE. Alternative: also a gym drop.     REC: Shop only, 300
     (a coin sink with a fixed price; a drop would make respecs luck-based)

 G4  Stock: the save keeps how many of each you own (save.evBerries),
     up to 999 each. Absent in old saves = 0, so no migration.  REC: yes

 G5  Where: the Team screen, any copy (team, locked, favorite included),
     outside runs only. A Lower button on the EVs row opens a panel; you
     pick counts per stat, then Lower asks once ("Lower Atk by 20 and Def
     by 10? These EVs are lost.") and saves. Cancel changes nothing.
     The button hides when the copy has no EVs.             REC: yes

 G6  The berries do nothing else (mainline also raises friendship;
     friendship has no effect here yet).                     REC: yes

 TESTS
=====================================================================

 NEW  docs/proposals/pokebox-evberries-harness.js   (picked up by check.js)
   Loads the real species-traits.js and copies.js; reads menu.js, gacha-ui.js
   and dev.html as text for the wiring. Hand-derived:
     1. The six berries: ids, names, stats in order HP to Spe, 10 each
     2. Buying: 300 coins each, coins down by 300, stock up by 1; poor
        refuses and changes nothing; 999 refuses
     3. Lowering: Atk 252 with 2 Kelpsy -> 232, 2 used; HP 5 with 1 Pomeg
        -> 0 (never below 0), 1 used; a stat at 0 refuses; asking for 3 with
        2 owned uses 2; total EVs drop by the same amount (510 -> 480)
     4. Lost, not refunded: after lowering, nothing else on the copy or the
        save gains EVs; a step 5 addEvs can fill the room again
     5. Missing copy or bad id: refused, berries untouched
     6. Save: no evBerries reads 0; clean() drops unknown ids and clamps to
        whole 0 to 999; a round trip keeps the stock
     7. Wiring: the Shop Items tab buys through buyBerry; the Team panel
        lowers through lowerEvs after one G.UI.ask, then commits; the
        Lower button only shows with EVs
   Why new: the EV harness covers earning and giving; buying and lowering
   are a new save field and new rules.

 NEW  docs/proposals/pokebox-evberries-mutate.js   (mutation runner, by hand)
   Each must make the harness fail: -1 instead of -10; lowering past 0; a
   berry not taken; berries taken but EVs kept; the wrong stat lowered;
   EVs refunded to another stat; coins not charged; charged twice; 999 cap
   ignored; buying with too few coins; clean() keeps junk ids; the panel
   skips the confirm; the panel does not save.

 UNCHANGED  the step 4 and step 5 harnesses (lowerEvs is new code; addEvs,
   the banks and the profile do not change).
 NO check.js change. NO intent scenarios. NEW debug set: G.Debug.evberries(n).
