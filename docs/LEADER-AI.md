## The AI player

- [Approved] ONE MODULE. Every Player-entity leader (Gym Leaders now; Elite Four, Uber and World Boss when they become Player entities) is driven by one AI player module, one per leader team. Each leader's behavior is data in its def (js/data/gyms.js for gyms), never code per leader. It replaces G.Boss.GymBrain on the switched gyms (Misty, Brock) first; gyms 3 to 8 keep the old brain until they switch (slice 12 of the host-model plan). It is separate from the wild Director: no threat budget, no population control.
- [Approved] YOUR CONTROLS ONLY. The AI acts only through the controller your keyboard answers, all seven calls of G.PlayerEntity.IDLE: `axis` (heading), `dodge`, `shake`, `swapSlot` (swap mon), `swapDir`, `slots` (slot keys), `cycles`. It never calls a cast, a swap or a move effect directly. Anything a leader does that a player cannot do is an authored difference (COMBAT-RULES "Host model", LEADER PRINCIPLE), never a hidden power of the AI. Triggers (below) also act through these calls. Shake-off: a leader has your accessibility setting `autoShake` on (3 taps per second, as a player with that setting), so `shake` answers nothing (2026-11-09-42-leader-auto-shake-is-your-accessibility-setting).
- [Approved] YOUR RULES. Its inputs obey your rules: the 0.4 s slot hold, the swap lock and trap lock, send-in i-frames, no swap in the air, the dodge's two charges and cooldowns, stuns that block input. A refused request is a no-op, as yours is. A stun refuses what it refuses for you (movement, dodge, a new attack: the Body's refusals, FRAME) and nothing else: slot presses and mon swaps land under your rules, timers, heal over time and commitments keep running, and a stun mid wind-up cancels it (the move stays ready, its freshness restored). The AI keeps deciding and pressing while stunned, with no stun check of its own; it may still see the stun on screen (its swap triggers read it). (Jess 2026-11-09, decisions 2026-11-09-a-stunned-leader-keeps-its-timers-running-like-you and 2026-11-09-every-player-entity-runs-one-flow-decide-hands-body-sim)
- [Approved] FRAME. Every Player entity runs four roles in one flow, each blind to the others' jobs (GLOSSARY Decider, Controller, Body, SimPass):
  - Decider (you, or the AI): takes in the screen only (the AI's eyes are G.LeaderInfo, plus its own HUD as you see yours) and answers commands to the Controller. It decides and enforces no rule. It keeps no stun check of its own: its tell and commitment gate (TICK) holds while stunned too.
  - Controller (your hands, or the AI's controller): carries the answers to the Body as requests; decides and filters nothing.
  - Body (the mon on the field): holds the state (HP, stats, statuses, stages, slots, dodges, locks) and checks each request against its acceptance rules (statuses, locks, authored "allowed to do" restrictions such as `autoShake` off or `firstWait`); what it accepts goes to the SimPass. A refused request passes nothing on (no "not moving"), so the SimPass never learns why something did not happen. It decides nothing and changes no state itself.
  - SimPass: resolves what the Body accepted and everything nobody asked for (timers, damage and heal over time, commitments, other entities' hits), updating the game state; it never asks why. Authored "how it plays out" differences live here (the tell before release, the enrage).
  - Code order: the Controller answers, then the Body, then the SimPass. Yours (js/game.js): the slot keys (takeSlotInput), the swap point (the team's swap lock, then handleSwap), the shake (shakeStep), the body (pl.update), the slot hold (tickSlotLock), your sim pass (G.MoveRoute.tick). A leader's (G.GymLeader update): its Decider (G.LeaderAI.think), then the same steps (the swap point is G.GymArena.swapStep; a swap hands the rest of the frame to the incoming mon, fieldFrame; its sim pass is G.GymLeader.SimPass.frame), checked step by step in docs/proposals/player-move-trace-harness.js (#43 step B). A leader's frame takes this order exactly; only authored differences change it (COMBAT-RULES "Host model", LEADER PRINCIPLE, authoring layers).
  - Built in #47a (2026-11-09, decision 2026-11-09-42-leader-attacks-wait-on-your-cast-gate): the leader's reach check is gone. Its SimPass asks your cast gate (G.MoveSim.gateOpen: the targeting, cost and dash rules your cast runs) before a wind-up starts, and a refusal by the Body or the gate waits your 0.05 s retry (G.MoveSim.RETRY), as yours. So a shot starts inside its range (not only as far as it flies) and a nova anywhere; the tell (its aim locked at the start, its release whether or not you are still there) stays the authored difference. Test: docs/proposals/leader-cast-gate-harness.js (mutation check: leader-cast-gate-mutate.js); the 5a parity trace was re-recorded (Brock's wind-ups up to 2 frames later, the retry grid).
  - Built in #43 step A (2026-11-09, behavior-neutral): the Body's checks are `acceptHeading`, `acceptDodge` and `acceptsCast` on every Player entity (a leader's `acceptsAttack` builds on `acceptsCast`); G.MoveSim asks `acceptsCast` before a cast; the movement, the dodge's travel, the timers and the over-time effects run in the SimPass step G.PlayerEntity.SimPass (js/entities/player-sim-pass.js), inside the update in its old order (decision 2026-11-09-43-movement-split-opening). Built in #43 step B (2026-11-09): a leader's slot hold, swap lock, mon swap and shake run on your path in your order (Code order above); the Misty loop 2 parity trace was re-recorded (its swap lands one frame earlier).   (Jess 2026-11-09, decisions 2026-11-09-every-player-entity-runs-one-flow-decide-hands-body-sim and 2026-11-09-four-roles-decider-controller-body-sim-pass)
- [Approved] THE AI CHOOSES ITS ATTACKS (Jess 2026-11-09, decision 2026-11-09-the-leader-ai-chooses-its-attacks). Which move a leader casts is the Decider's choice, made by G.LeaderAI and pressed as slot keys; the Body only accepts or refuses. A self heal is the AI's choice too: being unhurt sharply lowers its score (your rule casts it whenever due; the per-fight heal cap stays authored). The tell stays an authored SimPass difference. Built in #41: a bonded group fires together, as yours (its first due member leads the wind-up); unhurt, the Decider moves off a slot holding only a self heal.
- [Approved] VISIBLE. Every AI action reads on screen as the same action of yours would (a swap, a dodge, a stance change), never as a hidden number.

## What it can see (the information model)

- [Approved] THE SCREEN ONLY. The AI reads what a human would see on screen: positions, projectiles and hazards in flight, attack tells, HP bars, types, statuses and stage markers, and both rosters. It never reads live cooldown timers, hidden numbers (cards, IVs, EVs, nature, exact stats), RNG or your next input. One information layer produces everything the AI and its memory read, so nothing can reach the AI around it.
- [Approved] ROSTERS. Both sides see the other's full roster from the fight's start: each mon's species and whether it is active, on the bench or fainted. The player side gets a matching leader-roster UI (code differs: not built).
- [Approved] REVEALS, per mon of yours (keyed by its copy's `pokemonUUID`, which a fighter carries as `copyUid`; the team slot stands in when it is null):
  - a move is revealed the first time it is cast on screen ("on screen" means cast at any point since the fight's start, Jess 2026-11-08; no camera check), and then the AI knows everything about it: type, category, power, shape, effects and its cooldown length ([Legacy behavior] until #49 slice 1: the approved rule is the learned cooldown window, see "The Decider (#49)"). Never how much of its live timer is left;
  - a bond is revealed when two different moves of one mon are cast with no slot switch seen between them (Jess 2026-11-08). Only the active slot casts, so both came from one slot; the switch is visible (your setActive ring), so the AI may know it. No time window: a bond whose moves have unlike cooldowns still reveals. Never read from move data or the SimBus;
  - a held item is revealed when its effect shows (a Leftovers heal, a Focus Sash save). Code differs: split out of slice 1 to issue #36 (it may ride an item rework);
  - cards, IVs, EVs, nature and stats are never revealed.
- [Approved] STALE REVEALS. In a later fight of the same run a remembered move still counts as revealed, until it is seen to be missing or for that mon's first 10 s on the field (TUNE), whichever ends first. New moves or items must be revealed again.
  - [Approved] (Jess, 2026-11-08, slice 4 feel test) "Seen missing" means that mon has cast a whole move set (as many different moves as it carries, 4) this fight without the remembered one.

## Run memory and behavior tracking

- [Approved] SCOPE. Each leader remembers only its own fights, for the rest of the run. Nothing is shared between leaders and nothing carries into the next run. Per-fight state (field time, swaps used, who was saved at low HP) lives on the AI, never on the mons.
- [Approved] STORAGE. In memory only, run-scoped like Silver: created empty at G.Game.startRun, dropped at endRun, never written to the save (a run has no mid-run resume, so there is nothing to migrate). The leader entity is rebuilt every fight, so the store lives outside it and each fight's leader reads and writes its own record.
  - One record per leader, keyed by gym id.
  - Inside it, one entry per mon of yours (keyed as in REVEALS): its revealed moves, bonds and held item, each with when it was last confirmed; its behavior counts; the reason tags counted against its swaps.
  - One team section: patterns across your team (your usual lead, who you send in after a faint).
  - Only observations are stored. Which patterns a leader believes and exploits is derived from the counts with that leader's settings at each fight, so retuning a leader's learning applies to old observations too.
- [Approved] WHAT IS TRACKED, per mon and per team: slot and bond choices (how often you switch, which you favor); mon swaps (how often, out of which mon, into which); dodges (when, how often); your fighting range.
- [Approved] WHY YOU SWAPPED. Every swap is tagged with the visible context at that moment: your HP was low, you were at a type disadvantage, you were trapped or carried a status, the active slot was on cooldown, a leader mon had just been sent in. The tags are counted per mon ("swaps out under 30% HP 4 times in 5"). Plain rules, no machine learning.
  - Built (slice 4, #33; the lines are TUNE, BALANCE.md "Leader AI"): low is under 30% HP; a disadvantage is the leader's mon on the field having a type that hits yours super effectively; a status is a major status or a trap; the cooldown is every move of your active slot recharging (the HUD ring); "just sent in" is within 2 s. A send-in after your faint is counted as who you send in, not as a swap.
- [Approved] THE FIRST EXPLOIT (Jess, 2026-11-08, slice 4): your usual lead. Each fight books one lead sighting, the mon of yours with the most field time; a leader believes the pattern once one mon has `learnRate` sightings and no other ties it. On the fight's first free think tick it asks the prediction cap; when that says act, the `exploit` trigger fires and it swaps to its best matchup against that mon without waiting out `minField` (it counts as a voluntary swap), or stays when it is already on it and still says the line. The other tracked habits are not acted on yet. The exploit line goes ahead of the line queue.
- [Approved] FEED. Observations come only from the information layer, at the moment the thing shows on screen (a cast, a swap, a dodge, an item effect).
- [Approved] ROLLING WINDOWS, per fight: averages over a short window (about 2 s) and a long one (about 15 s, TUNE; approved 10 s, built in #50) of damage it deals and takes, both HP trends, your movement (speed, standing still) and the time since it last landed a hit. They feed confidence and pacing.
- Later, not now: a mid-run resume would save this store with the run (every field null-checked with a default, CLAUDE.md "Save-data migration safety"). Co-op would key entries per human player too; deferred (CLAUDE.md "Local-first").

## The decision loop

- [Approved] TICK. The AI decides on a tick (the `think` interval, baseline 0.25 s, TUNE), never inside a tell or a move commitment. The reflex delay applies on top to anything it reacts to.
- [Approved] TELL READING (Jess, 2026-11-08, after the slice 2 feel test). "Inside a tell" means its wind-up, until the move fires; the 0.35 s recovery after it is not the tell. A think tick that lands inside a tell or a commitment is not skipped: its choice waits for the first frame out of it. Why: at close range GymBrain is in a tell or its recovery about 86% of the time, so with the recovery counted and ticks skipped Misty never swapped and most dodges expired.
- [Approved] HARD GATES FIRST, never scored: your rules (Team.canSwap, tryDodge) and the AI's own gates (the stall guards below, no tell, no commitment). [Open] The full list of AI gates is defined in the slice that first needs each one.
- HISTORY (the slot and pacing choices removed by #51, the swap choice by #52, both built 2026-11-09; see "The Decider (#49)") SCORED CHOICES. Each open action (STAY, a swap to bench mon i, a dodge; until #51 also a slot and a pacing change) gets a score from considerations times the leader's settings. STAY has a baseline, and an action must beat it by a margin, so nothing fires without a reason and choices do not flicker.
- [Approved] DETERMINISTIC RATES. Rates and the prediction cap are fixed counters ("acts 7 times in 10"), not dice. `randomness` draws from a random source seeded per fight; a harness fixes the seed, so every outcome stays hand-derivable.
- [Approved] THREAT, for dodging: one of your live shapes whose path crosses its body soon.
- [Approved] DODGE DIRECTION (Jess, 2026-11-08). Sideways to the threat's path, to the side with more open floor. Later, aggression and confidence lean it: the more aggressive or confident, the more it angles toward you to close distance; the less, the more it angles away. Built (slice 3, #32): it turns by (effective aggression - 0.5) x 2 x `lean` degrees (baseline 45), never past you.
- HISTORY (removed by #52, built 2026-11-09: it is the Dash in action, the extra charge kept back a cost; see "The Decider (#49)") GAP-CLOSING DODGE (Jess, 2026-11-08). With enough effective aggression (baseline 0.7, TUNE) a leader may also dodge with no threat at all, purely to close distance so a short-range move of its own comes into reach. It spends a charge like any dodge, so `dodgeReserve` keeps charges back for threats. It also always keeps one more charge back, even with `dodgeReserve` 0 (Jess, 2026-11-08, decision B). Built (slice 3, #32): on the think tick, with a close move ready and you outside its reach but within one dodge of it, straight at you. #51: breathers are gone, so that condition went with them; it rides the dodges switch.
- [Approved] DODGE READ BAG (Jess, 2026-11-08; replaces the same day's roll with a missed-dodge bonus). `dodgeRead` is a shuffle bag: each block of 10 threats holds exactly that many reads (baseline 6), in an order shuffled from its own per-fight seeded source (separate from `randomness`'s). No streak cap: a run across a block join may be longer (Jess, 2026-11-08). Exact over every block, never certain, hand-derivable with the seed fixed. One threat is one of your casts with shapes in flight (a fan counts once). Built in slice 2 (#31).
- [Approved] THREAT TIMING (Jess, 2026-11-08). Threats are noticed on any frame, not only on the think tick; a read threat is answered exactly `reflex` later. A threat is a shape whose path reaches its body within 0.6 s (TUNE). Swaps and every other choice wait for the tick.
- [Approved] Dodges run in every switched fight, loop 1 single-mon fights included (Jess, 2026-11-08).

## The Decider (#49)

Source: the #49 design session, Jess 2026-11-09 (decisions 2026-11-09-49-decider-is-utility-goals-plus-goap-planner and 2026-11-09-49-leader-sharpness-base-plus-loop-growth). Working notes, now history: docs/proposals/49-decider-design-notes.md. Code differs: slices 1 to 3 built (#50 Belief, #51 the planner core, executor and overlay, #52 swap, dash, draw and dodge, 2026-11-09); slice 4 not yet (Build order). Rules below are [Approved] unless tagged.

- [Approved] PRINCIPLE. Emergent, not scripted: every behavior comes out of a score (considerations x personality weights). No hard-coded hold times, break conditions, share caps or forced actions on top. A fairness limit that seems to need a hard rule is raised as a question.
- [Approved] SHAPE. The Decider is Perception (G.LeaderInfo) -> Belief -> goal choice (utility) -> planner (GOAP) -> executor, with a reflex layer that can interrupt. Its commands go to the Controller only (FRAME).
- [Approved] BELIEF. A per-fight module between G.LeaderInfo and the planner. It only derives; G.LeaderMemory stays the store of raw observations. It updates from events on any frame; the reflex delay applies only to actions. It holds your state (HP, statuses, stages, position, velocity), your moveset (revealed, stale, the own-type guess), inferred cooldowns, the opponent model, the self-assessment (confidence, the rolling windows: short 2 s, long 10 s) and its own exact state. Derived once for every reader: the matchup, danger (your likely next hit on its active mon) and opportunity (its best hit on you). Your position is a short straight-line extrapolation, with `aimError` on top.
- [Approved] COOLDOWN WINDOW (replaces "knows its cooldown length"). A reveal starts a window centered on the move's base cooldown with a starting width (TUNE). A clean observation is two casts of one move with its slot kept active between them. On a clean gap inside the window each edge closes on the gap by the leader's `memoryUse` factor, so higher learns faster (worked, with 0.5 and 0.8: 0.2 to 2.0 with a gap of 1.2 gives 0.7 to 1.6 and 1.0 to 1.36; decision 2026-11-09-50-cooldown-window-memoryuse-and-baseline). Code baseline: `memoryUse` 0.5, starting width 1.8 s, minimum width 0.2 s (TUNE). A gap outside it stretches the window to cover the gap plus an overshoot (the starting width scaled by `memoryUse`), then narrows the same way. A minimum width (TUNE) means it is never certain; that floor is the cooldown fairness limit (`predictCap` applies only to habit predictions). Readiness: not ready before the window, a ramp across it, ready after. An unrevealed move is believed ready. Learned gaps live in run memory with the reveal; no forgetting on a timer. Cast timing is fixed (a move fires when ready and its slot is active), so your counter-play is cycling slots, swapping and movement.
- [Open] Bench cooldowns: belief mirrors the real bench-recharge rule, which waits on Jess's bench-recharge call (it changes every Player entity, HumanPlayer too). Until then belief mirrors the live rule (a benched member's cooldowns do not tick, player.js benchUpdate) from one place, so the call changes both.
- [Approved] OPPONENT MODEL. Every prediction has one shape (what it predicts, how sure from counts and `learnRate`, whether it acts under `predictCap`). #49 builds only your usual lead (THE FIRST EXPLOIT).
- [Approved] GOALS. Nine, fixed in code; leaders only weight them. Each target is an outcome, never a position, so every plan ends in its payoff. Danger is never a threshold in a target; it enters only as cost. Each goal has a fixed cost profile in code (weights on damage taken, damage dealt, time; TUNE):
  - Endurance: land a hit. Taken high, dealt neutral, time very low (patient).
  - Maintain: land a hit from its preferred range (`range`). Neutral throughout.
  - Pressure: land a hit. Taken low, dealt high, time high (rushes).
  - Setback: land a status, or lower one of your stages. Neutral throughout.
  - Kite: land a hit from outside your reach. Taken very high, dealt normal, time low.
  - Buff: gain at least +1 stage. Taken high, dealt ignored, time low.
  - Bait: land a hit while your best revealed move is on cooldown. Taken very high, dealt high, time low.
  - Finish: your active mon fainted. Taken lowest, dealt high, time neutral.
  - Conserve: a resource recovered (heal, cooldown, dodge charge). Taken highest, dealt ignored, time low.
- [Approved] GOAL VALUE AND SCORE. One currency: HP traded as a fraction of the opponent's max HP. A goal's value is its utility (considerations read from Belief, x the leader's goal weight, with confidence raising Pressure and Finish and lowering Conserve and Endurance) x its base value (TUNE, in code). Buff is worth the extra damage the stage is expected to deal over the long window. Finish is worth your active mon's current HP fraction plus a KO bonus (about 0.3, TUNE); the KO bonus lives in Finish only. Conserve is worth what it recovers, so it scores nothing when nothing is spent.
- [Approved] COST. Expected damage taken (danger x duration) minus expected damage dealt (opportunity x hit chance), plus a small time term, each weighted by the goal's profile; `risk` multiplies the damage-taken weight; the action's tag multiplier (or its per-action override) multiplies the whole. A heal is negative damage taken, usable by any goal. Hit chance is one formula in code (distance against the shape's reach, your sideways speed against the shot, the shape's width, `aimError`; TUNE).
- [Approved] CHOICE. Every think tick it plans for every goal and ranks each (goal, plan) by goal value minus plan cost; the running plan adds its commitment bonus (what it has invested x a per-leader stubbornness weight, scaled by confidence toward the goals confidence favors); `randomness` draws from the ranking (seeded, fixed tie-breaks). When no plan of 4 actions reaches a goal, its best plan counts at value x the fraction of the gap it closes (partial credit).
- [Approved] PLANS. At most 4 actions, the cheapest plan per goal (A*). Replanned every think tick and at once when the running action's precondition breaks. Built (#51): the tree of plans up to 4 actions is walked whole instead of by A*, since costs can be negative (damage dealt is subtracted) and A* needs costs of 0 or more; it finds the same cheapest plan. A plan ends in its payoff: a longer plan past the step that reached a goal is no plan for that goal.
- [Approved] #51 BUILD CALLS (made while building; approved by Jess 2026-11-09, decision 2026-11-09-51-build-calls-approved): (a) Finish's partial credit is the KO bonus x the share of your HP dealt, with damage dealt left out of that plan's cost, since counting your HP in the value and the dealt in the cost made a 4-hit chain win every tick at any HP (a Finish plan that reaches the KO keeps the full rule); (b) each goal's partial-credit gap is the distance to where its target can be reached: Maintain needs an attack that reaches its range, Kite one that outreaches your best reach, Bait a revealed move of yours, else no partial credit; (c) Recover counts only what was spent before the plan began, so Conserve scores nothing when nothing is spent; (d) hit sizes (danger, opportunity, the planner's) read against the max HP the struck mon's HP bar shows, so a leader's hpMult counts (G.Stats.visibleHitFrac alone used the species formula and read your hits at about 117% of a leader mon's HP). Not yet modelled (code differs, see #57 and #58): Hold at range circling you does not lower your hit chance in the planner's danger (ACTIONS says it should, #57); your shots count from the closest distance an action reaches (#58).
- [Approved] ACTIONS. Ten verbs fixed in code, filled in per slot, mon or range. Each has preconditions, expected effects, a cost, a computed duration, a tag, and a steering routine that answers the Controller every frame. Every movement verb names the slot it keeps active (only the active slot fires; the cast gate fires a targeted move the moment a target is in its reach).
  - Close to reach (slot X), approach: distance within X's reach.
  - Hold at range (R), position: circle you at R.
  - Back off, retreat: outside your best revealed reach.
  - Dash in (slot X), dash: a dodge toward you; needs a charge beyond the reserve and X's reach within one dodge. The extra charge kept back is a cost.
  - Wait for a window, wait: until a named event (its slot ready, your window opens).
  - Draw your shot (your move M), approach: step into M's reach as its window opens, so M fires.
  - Dodge your shot (M), dodge: planned, but it fires on the reflex clock the moment the shot is noticed (after `reflex`, through the `dodgeRead` bag), never on the think tick. Its cost is your hit x (1 - its expected success), the success read through `dodgeRead` and `reflex` (decision 2026-11-09-52-opening-stall-guards-dodge-cost-swap-value).
  - Use slot X: activate X and stay in reach until it fires; its effects and tag (attack, status, buff, heal) come from the move's data.
  - Swap to mon Y, swap: the new matchup: the plan's later steps read the incoming mon (matchup, HP, moves) for danger and opportunity, the send-in i-frames and the swap lock in its cost; `swapWill` is its cost term; it is also worth its matchup over the long window (10 s), as Buff is valued: the change in your shots' damage per second on the mon on the field and in its best hit per second on you, signed (decision 2026-11-09-52-swap-matchup-window-and-duels); `minField` and `swapLimit` stay hard gates (STALL GUARDS); the lead exploit raises its value at fight start; only a plan's first step (decision 2026-11-09-52-opening-stall-guards-dodge-cost-swap-value).
  - Recover, wait: a spent cooldown or charge comes back.
  - An action ends on its effect or at 1.5 x its estimate (TUNE).
- [Approved] SIGNATURE ACTIONS. A leader may add named actions to its own pool: one base verb, plus extra preconditions from the shared list, plus its own cost multiplier, plus an optional line. Data only, no new code path (Brock's "Sandbag": Hold at range, own Defense at +1 or more, cost x0.6).
- [Approved] REFLEX. Threats no plan expected stay reflex (THREAT TIMING, the DODGE READ BAG decides noticing); spending a charge is a score weighted by the current goal. After any dodge the plan resumes if its preconditions hold, else it replans at once. Built (#52): a reflex dodge that would spend down to `dodgeReserve` goes only when your hit x the goal's damage-taken weight x `risk` reaches the kept-back worth (your best hit x 0.5); a planned Dodge your shot is the watch's answer to its own move, without that check. Built (#59, decision 2026-11-09-59-reflex-weighs-the-plan-bait-parked): while the running plan still has a Dodge your shot step ahead, a reflex dodge that would spend the charge it needs goes only when that same hit beats the plan's worth (its value minus its cost), since spending it breaks the plan.
- [Approved] MISJUDGMENT. `misjudge`: seeded noise on each candidate plan's cost, drawn once per plan per think tick. The leader thinks a bad plan is good, unlike `randomness`, which picks a worse goal on purpose.
- [Approved] SHARPNESS AND STYLE. Sharpness settings take a per-leader base plus a fixed per-loop growth step, held inside each setting's valid range: `misjudge` [Approved]; `memoryUse`, `reflex`, `dodgeRead` [Proposed] (Jess: "likely"). Style settings (goal weights, tag multipliers, signature actions, `range`, `swapWill`, `risk`) stay flat per leader. Run memory (learning you during a run) is separate.
- [Approved] DEFAULT LEADER. Every goal weight 1, every tag multiplier 1, no signature actions, sharpness at the code baseline. Misty and Brock run it until #39 authors them.
- [Approved] OVERLAY (?dev). The current goal, the plan's steps with each cost, the top three alternatives (value and cost), the cooldown-window bar for each of your revealed moves, and the misjudgment noise on the chosen plan. A ?dev G.Debug toggle pauses on Escape without the pause menu, so the overlay can be read on a frozen frame.
- [Approved] TESTS (#49's end-goal test, seeded headless duels, plus Jess's feel test): Bait emerges (against a Charmander that stays on Ember, Brock completes a Draw, Dodge, Use plan and lands hits during Ember's cooldown); learning (the punish rate in fight 3 of a run beats fight 1); sharpness (loop 6 Brock wins more seeded duels than loop 1 Brock); counter-play (cycling slots gets punished less than staying on one); determinism (one seed, one trace). ## Fairness: smart, not oppressive

- [Approved] HUMAN LIMITS are the main fairness levers, all authored per leader: a reflex delay before it responds to anything it sees (dodging, aiming; baseline 0.15 s, TUNE), the think interval, aim error, and a dodge that reads a threat only after the reflex delay and only with a charge left.
- [Approved] PREDICTION CAP. A pattern the leader believes is acted on at most a capped share of the time (default about 70%, TUNE; a very hard leader can author up to about 95%), and still waits out the delays. A prediction is never certain, so you can bait it.
- [Approved] STALL GUARDS. A voluntary swap needs `minField` seconds on the field first (baseline 8 s; a faint swap and Re-Do start the clock too), and at most `swapLimit` voluntary swaps per fight (absent no cap, 0 never).
- [Approved] TELLS stay as an authored difference, on by default for every gym (the existing `tells` restriction); a gym may shorten or remove them.
- [Approved] NO RUBBER BANDING. The leader never changes stats, HP, damage, tempo, cooldowns or any authored number, and never secretly eases off when you are losing or presses when you are winning. Difficulty comes only from its settings and build. Confidence and pacing change behavior only.

## Authoring a leader

- [Approved] ONE FLAT LIST OF SETTINGS, no presets. A setting a leader does not author falls back to one baseline value set in code. Any setting may be keyed per loop, like the build keys. Starter list:
  - Reaction: `reflex` (seconds), `think` (seconds).
  - `aimError`.
  - Dodge: `dodgeRead` (how often it reads a threat), `dodgeReserve` (charges it keeps back).
  - Style: `aggression` (also drives pacing, below), `range` (preferred distance), `risk`, `buffBias`, `swapWill`.
  - Swaps: `minField`, `swapLimit` (the swap thresholds went with the old swap choice in #52; `swapWill` is the swap's cost multiplier, baseline 1).
  - Memory: `memoryUse` (how much its choices lean on revealed moves and your patterns), `learnRate` (how many observations before it believes a pattern), `exploit` (how hard it acts on a believed pattern), `predictCap` (the prediction cap).
  - `randomness` (0 to 1): how often it tries an option that is not its best, so it does not play like a machine.
  - Confidence: `confStart`, `confSwing`, `confMin`, `confMax`.
  - Decider (#49, code differs until its slices): `misjudge`; a weight per goal; a cost multiplier per action tag with optional per-action overrides; signature actions; a stubbornness weight. Sharpness settings take a base plus a per-loop growth ("The Decider (#49)", SHARPNESS AND STYLE).
- HISTORY (the slot choice removed by #51, the swap choice by #52, both built 2026-11-09: a swap is the planner's Swap action, the thresholds gone into its scores; see "The Decider (#49)") SWAP AND SLOT CHOICES weigh the matchup and your revealed moves, scaled by `memoryUse`. Voluntary swaps follow your swap rules, for hard CC, low HP, matchup, a trap or a buff setup, as often as `swapWill` and confidence allow. The slice 9 v2 swap triggers are considerations, with their numbers as the baseline thresholds (TUNE): asleep or frozen with 1.5 s or more left (to the healthiest bench mon); under 25% HP with no self heal left, once per mon (to the best matchup at 50% HP or more); a bench matchup 2x the active's or better (that mon, at 40% HP or more).
- [Approved] MATCHUP, per mon m: offense = its best open damaging move's type x your active mon's types x STAB 1.5; defense = max(0.25, your attack's type x m's types); score = offense / defense. Your attack is your active mon's most threatening revealed move; before any has shown, the AI guesses an attack of your mon's own type (Pikachu: Electric), replaced as soon as a real move shows. Ties (Jess, 2026-11-08): "most threatening" is the highest type multiplier against m, then the higher power; for a two-type mon with no reveal, the guess is the type that hits m harder. Worked: you Bulbasaur with Vine Whip, Staryu 1 / 2 = 0.5, Seel 2 / 2 = 1.0, so Seel comes in after `minField`; you Charmander with Ember, Staryu and Seel both 3 / 0.5 = 6, so no swap.
- [Approved] CONFIDENCE is a runtime value, moved by fight events and the rolling windows: up on hits landed and an HP or kill lead, down on hits taken and its own faints. Low confidence shifts the style settings toward caution and swapping; high toward aggression. Authored: start, swing, floor and ceiling.
  - (Jess, 2026-11-08, slice 3.) One value per leader team, 0 to 1 (baseline start 0.5, floor 0.2, ceiling 0.9, TUNE), not per mon; its own faint drops it. It shifts an EFFECTIVE AGGRESSION (the authored `aggression` nudged up or down by confidence), which is what pacing, the dodge lean and the gap-closing dodge read, and it shifts the swap choice (built as the STAY margin: lower when unsure, higher when sure; there is no separate `swapWill` setting yet). The other style settings (`range`, `risk`, `buffBias`) wait for slice 5.
  - Built (slice 3): what moves it is only what G.LeaderInfo shows: hits from the damage events (fractions of the struck mon's max HP), faints, and the long window's trade. The numbers: BALANCE.md "Leader AI".
- HISTORY (removed by #51, built 2026-11-09: replaced by the goals, the 25% breather cap dropped; see "The Decider (#49)") PACING, driven by effective aggression. It is what you see: at any moment the leader is in one stance, breather, neutral or push. It is a visible stance change, never a hidden number. Per leader team, not per mon (Jess, 2026-11-08).
  - PUSH: the close stance, walking in to attack. It starts on frustration (a long spell without landing a hit; baseline 6 s, sooner at higher effective aggression) or to press an advantage (high confidence while you are hurt).
  - BREATHER (Jess, 2026-11-08, replaces "far stance, no new tell"): after sustained pressure on you the leader backs off into the far stance and, when the active mon has a buff or heal slot, picks it with the slot keys. Nothing else: moves fire automatically and your controls have no hold fire, so it never withholds a cast. Self-buffs and heals still cast. Higher effective aggression means shorter, rarer breathers.
  - A breather has a maximum share of the whole fight's time (baseline 25%, TUNE); a swap does not reset it. A mon sent in arrives in neutral, which ends a breather.
- [Approved] TRIGGERS, a few per leader (their forced slot and swap actions were removed by #51, built 2026-11-09: the gyms.js validator refuses them and a forced-action hook stays as an unused stub; lines and `set` stay, decision 2026-11-09-51-opening-stance-traces-and-set-trigger; see "The Decider (#49)"), each once unless marked repeating.
  - Conditions: fight start; its own HP crossing a line; a faint (yours or its own); you sending in a mon; time elapsed (slice 3, #32); starting to exploit a pattern (`exploit`, slice 4, #33).
  - Actions (through your controls): choose a slot, swap to a mon, change a setting for a while, say a line.
  - Built (slice 3; the stall-guard skip approved by Jess, 2026-11-08): authored as `ai.triggers` in js/data/gyms.js (the shape is in the comment above AI_KEYS there; the validator checks it). A trigger waits out a tell or commitment, as every choice does. A slot press obeys your slot hold and a swap your swap rules (lock, trap, Fly); a trigger's swap does not wait for the AI's own stall guards (`minField`, `swapLimit`), since it is authored, but it counts as a voluntary swap. An HP trigger fires when the mon on the field crosses its line from above.
- [Approved] LINES. A leader says short authored lines on screen at triggers and, optionally, on a large confidence swing (in from slice 3, with a cooldown so they do not spam; Jess, 2026-11-08) ("Brock: Let's get serious!"; "You always pull Pikachu when it's hurt, don't you?"). A voluntary swap always shows the ring plus "<leader> switches to <mon>!", with no camera kick and no boss sound. New strings are English only (CLAUDE.md "English-native i18n").
  - Built (slice 3; Jess, 2026-11-08, decision C, the 4 s wait approved at the feel test): a line shows as "<leader>: <line>" on the banner. While another banner is up it waits, at most 4 s, then it is dropped. Swing lines are `ai.lines` { up, down }.
