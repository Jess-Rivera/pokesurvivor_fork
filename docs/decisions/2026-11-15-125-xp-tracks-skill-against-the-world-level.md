# 2026-11-15, #125: your level against the world level follows your skill, with diminishing returns ahead (Jess)

Today you outlevel the world by about Lv 4. Ruling, three bands of play:
- A poor or slow player slowly lags the world level until overwhelmed, then restarts stronger through meta
  progression bought with the currency they earned.
- An average player roughly matches the world level, or outpaces it very, very slowly.
- A good player stays ahead but does not pull away: the lead has diminishing returns.
XP and the economy are separate systems (see 2026-11-15-124-economy-independent-of-xp).
