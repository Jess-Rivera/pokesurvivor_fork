## 2026-11-08, Leader AI: an AI player through your controls (Jess)
Fresh design by grilling (the 2026-11-07 proposal docs/proposals/gym-leader-ai-design.md is frozen and compared afterwards). One AI player module drives every Player-entity leader; it acts only through your inputs (heading, slot key, swap-mon key, dodge) under your rules, and sees only the screen: both rosters, positions, tells, HP, types, statuses, and your moves as they are revealed per mon (keyed by pokemonUUID). It remembers its own fights for the rest of the run (in memory, never saved): reveals plus behavior counts with visible-context reason tags for your swaps. Fairness comes from authored human limits (reflex, think interval, aim error, dodge read) and a prediction cap (about 70%, up to about 95%); tells stay on by default; no rubber banding. Authoring is one flat settings list with a code baseline, no presets, plus confidence, triggers and English-only lines. Checked by a decision overlay, a limits harness and Jess's feel test. Built in five slices.
