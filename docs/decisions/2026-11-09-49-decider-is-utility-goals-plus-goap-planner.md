## 2026-11-09, #49: the Decider is utility goals plus a GOAP planner (Jess)
The Decider is a hybrid: utility scoring picks a goal, a GOAP planner builds a plan, an executor runs it through your controls, and a reflex layer (threat dodges) can interrupt. The three managers and the Arbiter are superseded. Nine goals fixed in code (Endurance, Maintain, Pressure, Setback, Kite, Buff, Bait, Finish, Conserve), each with an outcome target and a cost profile in code; danger enters only as cost, never as a threshold. Every think tick it plans for every goal and ranks (goal, plan) pairs by goal value minus plan cost, both in HP traded as a fraction of the opponent's max HP, read from Belief.
Actions are ten verbs fixed in code, filled in per slot, mon or range (Close to reach, Hold at range, Back off, Dash in, Wait for a window, Draw your shot, Dodge your shot, Use slot, Swap to mon, Recover); Use slot reads its effects from the move's data. A leader may add signature actions: one base verb plus extra preconditions and its own cost multiplier (data). Personality multiplies costs per action tag (11 tags), with an optional per-action override. Plans hold at most 4 actions; it replans every think tick (with the commitment bonus) and at once on a broken precondition. Swaps, the gap-closing "dash in" and Bait's planned dodge are planner actions; the planned dodge fires on the reflex clock when the shot is noticed. Unplanned threat dodges stay reflex.
