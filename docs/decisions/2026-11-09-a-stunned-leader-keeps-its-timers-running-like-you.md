## 2026-11-09, A stunned leader keeps its timers running, like you (Jess)
Found on slice 5a (#34): while a Player-entity leader is stunned its frame does not run, so its move timers, heal over time and commitments pause; yours keep running while you are stunned. Ruling: no authored difference here. A stunned leader behaves exactly as a stunned player does: the stun blocks its inputs only, and its timers, heal over time and commitments keep running.
Same day, after a GymLeader vs HumanPlayer audit: the fix is structural. The leader's frame takes your shape (controller answers, then your sim pass every frame, then the body); the slot press leaves the cast rules; the stun check lives only in the body. A stun mid wind-up cancels it (the attack does not fire, the move stays ready). The hard-coded 1.6 s first-attack wait becomes an authored per-gym setting (baseline 1.6 s).
