## 2026-11-11, #82 and #88: the Poke Ball pause menu (Jess)
Jess's design doc and mockup set the pause menu: dim, a grey circle with a Poke Ball that opens, four panels around it (Run Info, Pokemon detail, team, Run Inventory). Feel tests on #88 ruled: the ball opens while the panels slide in (about 0.23 s), closing is the reverse (about 0.16 s); the caps sit above the buttons; closed, a white centre button fills the notch hole; buttons are the run screen's move slot shape at 1.5x, flat body; ball centred, Run Info as big as the detail panel. End Run asks inside the ball, no popup: Resume and Settings slide off opposite random sides, End (red) and No (white) chase into Settings' row; focused answers keep their rim and turn their body the rim's dark shade; Move_Left / Move_Right switch, Esc answers No.
Co-op keeps the old pause screen (multiplayer deferred). The end-of-run Team screen moves to the new panels in #92.
