## 2026-11-09, Four roles: Decider, Controller, Body, SimPass (Jess)
Tightens the flow of 2026-11-09-every-player-entity-runs-one-flow-decide-hands-body-sim after terms drifted (decider, mind, hands, brain, AI, AI player). Every Player entity runs four roles, each blind to the others' jobs:
- Decider: you or the AI. Takes in the screen only (your eyes; the AI's eyes are G.LeaderInfo, plus its own HUD as you see yours) and answers commands to the Controller. Decides; enforces no rule.
- Controller: your hands, or the AI's controller. Carries the Decider's answers to the Body as requests; decides and filters nothing.
- Body: the mon on the field. Holds the state (HP, stats, statuses, stages, slots, dodges, locks) and checks each request against its acceptance rules (statuses, locks, authored "allowed to do" restrictions); what it accepts goes to the SimPass to resolve; a refusal passes nothing on (the SimPass still runs knockback, ice and poison on a stunned mon, and never learns why a request did not come). Decides nothing and changes no state itself.
- SimPass: resolves accepted requests and everything nobody asked for (timers, damage and heal over time, other entities' hits), updating the game state. Authored "how it plays out" differences (a tell before release, the enrage) live here.
The AI keeps no stun check of its own (its tell and commitment gate holds while stunned too); the Body refuses what a stun refuses. Code differs: the body's update still carries out its own movement and dodge travel, and the stun's refusal of a new attack sits in the SimPass (#43); the firstWait check sits in the SimPass (#41).
