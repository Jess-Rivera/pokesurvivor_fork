## 2026-11-27, #141 round R rulings (Jess)
Rollout ramps: each cast in a row doubles power up to x16, resetting after the 5th or a cast that hits nothing. Mimic becomes
the last move a leader hit you with, or a move of a wild target's type. Taunt makes hit foes rush you and lose their ranged
and status attacks (a leader loses its status moves). Dragon Cheer is a team-wide crit raise (+1, +2 on Dragon types).
Barb Barrage keeps its double power on poisoned foes (Hex's statusBonus, limited to poison). Hyper Drill's hits go through
shields and ignore damage reduction. Double Hit and Twin Beam need no ruling (quick repeats, as Double Kick). An import
batch joins the learnsets when Jess signs it off in the Move Review page. Durations and cooldowns stay [Open].
Manual aim stays out of the review tool (#175).
