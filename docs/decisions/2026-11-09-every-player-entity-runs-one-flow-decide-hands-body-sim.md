## 2026-11-09, Every Player entity runs one flow: decide, hands, body, sim pass (Jess)
Opening #40. Your frame is the model for every Player entity: the decider (you, or the AI) decides; the controller (your hands) carries the decision and decides nothing; the body tries to follow it under its rules and statuses (a stun, the slot hold, the swap lock); then the sim pass resolves rules and inputs and cares about nothing upstream. Four boundaries, each blind to the others' jobs: hands make no decisions, the mind does not set how long a status lasts, the sim never asks why. In code order: controller answers, then the body, then the sim pass (yours: takeSlotInput, pl.update, G.MoveRoute.tick). A leader's frame takes this order exactly; only authored differences change it.
Stuns follow from it: the body's rules alone decide what a stunned mon accepts. Yours accepts slot keys and mon swaps while stunned and refuses movement and dodge, so a leader's does the same; the AI keeps deciding and pressing, with no stun check of its own. A stun mid wind-up cancels it (2026-11-09-a-stunned-leader-keeps-its-timers-running-like-you); the cancelled move's freshness is restored and its warning visuals fade as a flinch's do.
