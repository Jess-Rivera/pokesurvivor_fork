## 2026-11-05, Player entities and Wild entities: architecture and the eleven host-model decisions (Jess)
New terms: Player entity (old strong host: Player, Gym Leader, World Boss, Uber), Wild entity (old weak host: Wild Enemy, Elite). Structure first: a `PlayerEntity` base (subclasses `HumanPlayer`, `GymLeader`, later `WorldBoss`, `Uber`) and a `WildEntity` base (subclass `Elite`), sharing reusable modules and one hit contract, chosen for extensibility and clean code over the smaller option C. HP is the player formula x an authored `hpMult` per gym and loop. Fight 60 s plus 30 s per extra mon, enrage restarts on send-out. No hit i-frames, no contact damage (authorable), one active slot. Voluntary swaps and dodge are core leader features. One fixed set of leader status rules with AI shake off at 3 taps per second (freeze 1 s, sleep 1 to 3 s, 5 s grace, TUNE). Elites do not change how they work: they inherit the wild rules and keep their current differences as mods, status cut included. Each family owns its attack and defense calculations. The Director stays a separate later topic, since it manages the wild population.
