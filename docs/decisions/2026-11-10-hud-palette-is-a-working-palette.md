## 2026-11-10, The HUD palette is a working palette, every colour labelled with a job (Jess)
The palette is one consistent set the HUD pulls from, not a frozen spec. Every colour carries a job label; colours can be added, relabelled or retired as the HUD is built, and a colour stays until we know it is unused.
The Panels and lines, Text, Bars and Status chips tables are adopted on that basis. Shake Off flashes Lv gold to white. Status chips cover the major statuses only; confusion, trap, daze, drowsy, slow and haste show as their on-sprite effects on the canvas, with no HUD chip.
