# 2026-11-21: ball centring keeps the wobble's lean; the tilt frames centre only up and down (Jess)

- Every Poke Ball frame is drawn with its filled pixels centred (centreShift, generated by tools/make_ball_sprites.py),
  everywhere BallSprite draws one: the Shop's cards and wells and the catch screen (#128 S6, D2).
- The wobble's tilt frames (frames 11 to 16, every wobble frame but the front face) take no sideways shift, so the
  wobble keeps its lean; their up and down shift stays, so the ball does not bob as the wobble passes the front face.
- Checked by docs/proposals/ball-sprites-harness.js.
