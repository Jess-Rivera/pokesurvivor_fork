## Stats and HP

- [Approved] Natures affect stats, mainline +10% and -10% on the final stat, rounded down in whole numbers, HP never (Jess 2026-11-03; built 2026-11-04, pokebox step 4: G.Stats.profile and the nature argument of statValue). Replaces the "nature ignored" of 2026-10-17.
- [Approved] Mainline stat formula with base + IV + EV + level. Other stats: `S = floor((2B + IV + floor(EV/4)) * L / 100) + 5`. HP: `HP = floor((2B + IV + floor(EV/4)) * L / 100) + L + 10`, then multiplied by HP_SCALAR (current value: see js/core/stats.js). (stat-model notes sections 2 and 4; DECISIONS 2026-10-17 Base-stats model)
- [Approved] The other five stats (HP, Atk, Def, SpA, SpD) scale with the player's IN-RUN LEVEL via the mainline formula (level plays the role of the Pokemon's level), so you get stronger as a run progresses. (DECISIONS 2026-10-17 Base-stats model)
- [Approved] SPEED is the deliberate exception: movement reflects the species' Speed IDENTITY and is constant within a run (the identity ratio r = Speed/SPEED_MEDIAN cancels level). It changes ONLY via items or upgrades, never from leveling up mid-run. Speed is the one stat SHAPED by the transfer function g(r) = exp(w * tanh(k * ln(r) / w)) with asymmetric bounds; the Speed stat itself stays raw everywhere else. Bounds, slope, SPEED_MEDIAN and the px/s at r = 1: current value: see js/core/stats.js (MOVE_MIN, MOVE_MAX, MOVE_K, SPEED_MEDIAN, BASE_PX) and BALANCE.md. (DECISIONS 2026-10-17 Base-stats model; stat-model notes sections 2, 4 and 6)
  - Doc gap: DECISIONS 2026-10-17 and stat-model notes still say bounds 0.25x to 2.0x; code and BALANCE.md use the later tuned bounds, now 0.7x to 1.3x since 2026-11-01 (Jeremy playtest; was 0.5x to 3.0x; DEVLOG-archive records the first retune as tuned by feel and the old wording as stale). See CP2 question 2.
- [Approved] Enemy Speed uses the same Speed-identity model as the player, fixed per species, with no in-run time ramp. Bosses keep their own spd path; the 'runner' behavior keeps its x1.6. (archive 2026-10-17 Enemy Speed = fixed species identity)
- [Approved] Enemy level comes from run time; species gating by enemy level only; fixed neutral enemy IVs. The level curve itself is owned by BOSS-REWORK.md. (stat-model notes section 4)
- [Approved] IV and EV are stored per copy of a Pokemon, not per species (Jess 2026-11-03, docs/proposals/pokebox-plan.md). IVs and the nature apply since pokebox step 4 (2026-11-04): a team slot's copy sets its fighter's profile (G.Copies.profileOf), Speed included through the movement curve; a missing copy plays neutral. Enemies stay at 0 IV and neutral (pokebox-plan step 4 D2). EVs apply since pokebox step 5 (2026-11-04): earned into six typed run banks (wild 1/1000, elite 1/500, gym 1x per yield point, shiny x2), given out at the end of a run, 252 per stat and 510 per Pokemon; spent EVs feed every stat, Speed through the movement curve. Enemies stay at 0 EVs. Design: docs/proposals/ev-progression.md.
- [Approved] Shedinja is fixed at 1 HP (bypasses everything), with Wonder Guard coming later via imported ability data. Code differs, see TODO (no Shedinja case in js/core/stats.js). (stat-model notes sections 2 and 4)
- [Approved] HP investment stays faithful (about half as potent as for other stats). (stat-model notes section 4)

## Damage

- [Approved] Player to enemy damage uses the real mainline damage formula, tuned toward survivor feel by ONE scalar: `base = ((2*L/5 + 2) * Power * (Atk/Def)) / 50 + 2`, then `* STAB(1.5) * type * crit * SCALAR`. SCALAR is DMG_SCALAR, current value: see js/core/stats.js. (DECISIONS 2026-10-17 Damage model)
- [Approved] L = attacker level (player's in-run level). Physical moves use Atk vs enemy Def; special moves use SpA vs enemy SpD, each scaled to level by statValue. Category comes from the move's `cat` via G.Stats.moveCat. (DECISIONS 2026-10-17 Damage model)
- [Approved] STAB = 1.5x when the attack type matches one of the ATTACKER's types, for every attacker and every target (you, wild enemies, Elites, gym leaders), applied after the +2 (the mainline spot). Flat damage gets none. Built in entity model slice 4b (2026-11-06). (Jess 2026-11-06, slice 4 H1; DECISIONS 2026-10-17 Damage model; combat-architecture D8)
- [Approved] Wild STAB is on (wild contact and shots use the enemy's first type), and the stand-in wild Power ENEMY_POWER goes 40 to 27 so horde damage stays about level. Built in slice 4b. (Jess 2026-11-06, slice 4 S1)
- [Approved] Damage scale by target: hits on the human take ENEMY_DMG_SCALAR, every other target (wild, Elite, gym leader) takes DMG_SCALAR. Values: js/core/stats.js. (Jess 2026-11-06, slice 4 H2)
- [Approved] Flat damage (terrain and liquids, bombs, Rocky Helmet, altars and chests, Fissure's KO, Dragon Rage, Night Shade) is outside the hit contract: no attacker half, no defense, no STAB. (Jess 2026-11-06, slice 4 H5)
- [Approved] Implementation is split (attacker half at cast, defender half at impact) but is mathematically identical to the single formula; the +2 stays after the division and before the STAB/type/crit/scalar multipliers. (DECISIONS 2026-10-17 Damage model)
  - Attacker half A = ((2L/5 + 2) x P x Astat) / 50 x extraMul. Astat = statValue(SpA or Atk at L). extraMul = atkMulAll x (1 + stat-card inc) x Atk/SpA stage multiplier x run-item multiplier. (combat-architecture D6; js/core/stats.js attackFactor)
  - Defender half D = (A / max(1, Dstat) + 2) x STAB x DMG_SCALAR. Dstat = statValue(Def or SpD at enemy level) x defender Def/SpD stage multiplier; status category uses PROP_DEF 50 and no STAB. (combat-architecture D7; js/core/stats.js applyDamage)
  - typeK = typeChart(moveType, enemyTypes) x weatherMult(moveType); Power Stone floors it at 1 for the held type. (combat-architecture D9)
  - dmg = max(1, D x typeK x itemDmgMul x critMul x handlerMul). (combat-architecture D11; handlerMul is move-path specific, see combat-architecture)
- History (removed in slice 7b-2b, 2026-11-01): the legacy path multiplied the defender half by the move's per-cast normalization `move.norm`. applyDamage has no normalization now (combat-architecture D7).
- [Approved] Floor: if reduction would make a hit 0, it does 1, except an immune (x0) hit, which does 0. (stat-model notes section 4)
- [Approved] Immunity overrides the floor of 1. Built in slice 4b. (Jess 2026-11-06, slice 4 I1; closes the stat-model notes section 4 Open item)
- [Approved] Crit: no base chance, +3% per Critical Hit card level, crit multiplies by 1.5. Move-based crit stages (12.5% / 50% / 100%) are DROPPED. (archive 2026-10-20 Status Slice D CLOSED; combat-architecture D10; CRIT_MUL in js/systems/combat.js)
- [Approved] Gym leader hits on the player crit by the same rule: the leader's Critical Hit card level plus the move's own crit percent, no base chance, x1.5 applied after the player's defense. With no crit cards authored, only moves with a crit percent (Psycho Cut 15%) crit today. (Jess 2026-11-01; js/systems/move-effects.js hurtPlayer)
- [Approved] The naked base-stat layer is intentionally upgrade-dependent: with base stats only, hits-to-kill a common enemy RAMPS over a run. This is BY DESIGN. Do not rebalance base stats to flatten it; tune the upgrade systems to fill the gap. The base layer's only job is a sane early hit-count (about 2 to 4) with a moderate ramp. (DECISIONS 2026-10-17 naked base-stat layer)

### Type chart

- [Approved] The full mainline chart (6th gen onward): x2 / x0.5 / x0. Dual types multiply; a combo with no immune type clamps to [x0.25, x4]; any immune type makes the hit x0. Used both ways (your attacks and attacks on you). Built in entity model slice 4b (js/data/types.js). (Jess 2026-11-06, slice 4 T1 to T3; lifts the QA-12 hold)
- [Approved] An immune hit: 0 damage, no side effects (no ailment, flinch, knockback, stat change, drain), no hurt feedback on the human (no i-frames, flash, sound, camera kick, Focus Sash, Substitute drain). It still counts as a hit (a shot stops on it, a chain can hop from it). A grey "Immune" text shows at most once per 0.5 s per target. Pure status moves keep their own immunity lists. (Jess 2026-11-06, slice 4 I2, I4)
- [Approved] Immunity is mainline on every target class: wild enemies, Elites, gym leaders and world bosses, no boss exception. Power Stone still pierces immunity for its type. (Jess 2026-11-06, slice 4 I3 = A)
- [Approved] Fixed-damage moves (Dragon Rage, Night Shade, Fissure) check immunity only, never resistance or weakness, as in mainline. (Jess 2026-11-06, slice 4 I5 = A)
- History: until slice 4b the chart was SOFTENED (x1.6 / x0.6 / x0.3, dual capped [x0.3, x2.4]). (QA-12)
- [Approved] Test dummies (the harness dummies hit by every move, and the debug test page's dummies) are typeless: x1 to every move, never immune. (Jess 2026-11-06, slice 4 D-T)

## Orthogonal dials

- [Approved] There are three independent scalars: (1) HP_SCALAR multiplies the HP formula and is SHARED by player AND enemy HP (G.Stats.hpForLevel); its job is HP consistency, nothing to do with damage. (2) DMG_SCALAR multiplies player to enemy DAMAGE; a damage dial. (3) An enemy to player damage scalar. Current values: see js/core/stats.js. (DECISIONS 2026-10-17 orthogonal dials)
- [Approved] RULE: move Power is a DAMAGE-side term. Changing it touches ONLY the damage dial, never HP. Migrating move power does NOT require re-solving enemy HP, player HP or HP_SCALAR. What does need re-solving after a power migration is the PLAYER DMG_SCALAR, re-centered once so the naked baseline still lands in the target hits-to-kill band against the UNCHANGED enemy HP curve. Power = relative balance BETWEEN moves; DMG_SCALAR = overall damage LEVEL. (DECISIONS 2026-10-17 orthogonal dials)
- [Approved] The enemy-damage scalar is solved against PLAYER HP (also unchanged): HP untouched, tune the damage dial. (DECISIONS 2026-10-17 orthogonal dials)

## G.Stats

- [Approved] All base-stats formulas live in ONE module, G.Stats (js/core/stats.js): statValue, HP (hpForLevel), Speed identity (speedToPx, moveTransfer), the enemy levelForTime curve, the tunable constants, and the Attack/Defense/SpA/SpD functions. Both the player and enemies call G.Stats; neither keeps its own copy. New combat-stat formulas, and any retuning of constants like HP_SCALAR or SPEED_MEDIAN, happen in G.Stats, not duplicated per entity. stats.js loads after core/util.js. (DECISIONS 2026-10-17 Stat formulas centralized)

## Swap rule

- [Approved] Every slotted move runs its own independent cooldown, active or not. Only the active group fires. The 35% incoming-wait cap in setActive is removed. (DECISIONS 2026-10-18 Swap rule decided)
- [Approved] A short hold after swapping stops instant back-and-forth swapping; the swapped-in move fires at once. Length: current value: see js/entities/player.js (SWAP_LOCK). (DECISIONS 2026-10-18 Swap rule decided)
- [Approved] Bond is reworked in its own branch (Slot Compression + Synergy). The old "fire several moves at once" role is gone once cooldowns are independent. (DECISIONS 2026-10-18 Swap rule decided)

## Dodge

- [Approved] Space (the rebindable "dodge" action) moves you 150 px in 0.5 s (300 px/s) along your heading, or your last heading when standing still. Heading and speed are locked for the dodge; confusion and daze bend the heading at the press only. (Jess 2026-11-02)
- [Approved] Two charges for the team (a swap never refills them). Each spent charge runs its own cooldown, about 5 to 10 s (current value: js/entities/player.js DODGE). Every dodge cooldown pauses while a dodge runs. The next dodge can start 0.2 s after the last one ends. (Jess 2026-11-02)
- [Approved] No i-frames. For the first 0.3 s the body circle is 20% of normal; every enemy hit test (shots, contact, hazards, leader shapes) uses that circle, so small shots miss more and narrow gaps fit. The body grows back once it fits where you stand. Walls stop a dodge; solid objects are slid around; enemies stay pass-through as always. (Jess 2026-11-02)
- [Approved] A dodge that ends on ice keeps 300 px/s until you leave the ice; the keys turn the heading at ice speed. (Jess 2026-11-02)
- [Approved] Frozen, asleep, flinched, pushed, rooted or carried by a move's dash: no dodge. A push, flinch, freeze or sleep cancels a dodge in progress (the charge stays spent). A move's dash waits while you dodge. (Jess 2026-11-02)

## Status effects

- [Approved] One status engine (G.Status, js/systems/status.js) for enemies, elites, bosses, gym leaders and the player. (archive 2026-10-19 Status Slice C)
- [Approved] Only one major status at a time: burn, poison (toxic), paralysis, sleep, freeze. Confusion and trap are not major and stack on top. (archive 2026-10-19 Status Slice C)
### Host model

- [Approved] LEADER PRINCIPLE (Jess, 2026-11-07; governs every rule below for Player-entity leaders, Gym Leader, Uber, World Boss): a leader is treated as just another player's team, controlled by AI. Anything a player can have (any upgrade card, team cards included, held and run items, IVs, EVs, nature, the player's attack and defense formulas), a leader can have. Every difference from a player is HAND AUTHORED per gym (the restrictions block, hpMult, authored cards and items), never a hidden built-in rule. When a rule here or in another spec gives leaders a fixed difference, read it against this principle and ask Jess (see the slice 6 map, "QUESTIONS"). Code differs, see the host-model plan: slices 6 to 12.
- [Approved] TERMS (Jess, 2026-11-05): Player entity replaces Strong Host and Wild entity replaces Weak Host. The rules below keep the old words until they are next edited. Decisions and slices: docs/proposals/host-model-plan.md.
- [Approved] LEADER RULES (Jess, 2026-11-05; built for Misty in entity model slice 5 and Brock at the entity model close-out 2026-11-07; gyms 3 to 8 differ until slice 12). A Gym Leader is a subclass of the `PlayerEntity` base. HP is the player formula x an authored `hpMult` per gym and loop, to make it feel like a boss. No contact damage unless authored, one active slot, voluntary swaps and dodge. Hit i-frames, the status rules, Shake off and the enrage are authored restrictions since slice 6 (next line); the enrage clock restarts on each send-out, for gyms that author one. One active slot is built since slice 8 (2026-11-07): one slot per authored move line (authored bonds share one), switched under your 0.4 s slot hold; pure buffs and self heals cast only from the active slot; the melee cushion and the flinch rule are yours. Voluntary swaps and dodge: code differs, see the plan's slices 9 and 10.
- [Approved] LEADER STATS, CARDS, ITEMS AND RESTRICTIONS (Jess, 2026-11-07, entity model slice 6; built for G.GymLeader, so Misty; old-body gyms differ until slice 12). Attack: the player's formula at the leader's level (IVs, EVs, nature, Protein and Calcium, held and run items, stages), times the enrage; the power knob must be 1. Build: per slot and loop, `ivs`, `evs`, `nature`, one held `item`; per gym and loop, up to 4 `runItems`. Cards: any card a player can get, team cards included (Vitamins as `vitamins:<stat>`, Gate and Duration, which change the leader's own stage timers); Regen too from slice 7b, at a loop that fields 2+ mons (it heals the bench, as yours). Items rejected on a leader, with the reason: Amulet Coin and Lucky Egg (human only), Snowball Badge (counts your kills), Choice Band and Specs (no move lock), Rocky Helmet (bugged, TODO). Life Orb costs the leader 1% of max HP per cast, as yours. Restrictions, each the player's rule unless authored: `enrage` (off), `statusCut` (freeze and sleep length multiplier, 1), `ccWindow` (no new hard CC, the player's 2.5 s), `autoShake` (on, 3 taps per second, since an AI has no hands), `hitIframes` (the player's 0.45 s), plus `tempo`, `tells` and `healLimit`. Card budget: a dev warning when the stacks pass level - 1, never a block. Misty authors the 2026-11-05 leader numbers: freeze 1 s, sleep 1 to 3 s, 5 s with no new hard CC, no hit i-frames, auto shake; no enrage. For every leader trap is the player's 8 s swap lock and there are no berry guards.
- [Approved] LEADER TEAM (Jess, 2026-11-07, entity model slice 7a; built for G.GymLeader, so Misty; old-body gyms stay one mon until slice 12). A switched gym fields its loop lineup (loop N, the first N roster slots) as one G.Team, the module your team uses, so it shares the same stores (team stages, team cards, run items, dodges, berries). Only the mon on the field is in the fight; the bench ticks as yours. When it faints, 1.3 s later the lowest living slot takes the fainted spot with your 0.5 s swap i-frames. Stages: as yours (SS13). Each fainted mon is a kill (EVs, Silver); the boss count, gym drop, badge and link come once, at the team wipe. HP is authored per loop as hpMult per mon (Misty: 25 at loop 1, 19 from loop 2). Each mon keeps its own enrage clock, started only by a hit while it is on the field (bench damage never starts it). The win line names the leader ("Gym Leader Misty Defeated!"). TEAM ITEMS (slice 7b, Jess 2026-11-07): Brass Knuckles, Sharp Scissors and Pikachu's Tear read the HOLDER's team (yours as before), with your 3+ rule (a dev warning when authored on fewer). Re-Do on a leader: at the wipe, no win; after the 1.3 s beat the whole team is back at full HP (your revive), each clock reset, the last to fall on the field; once per fight; a revived mon pays a kill again when it falls. Voluntary swaps: slice 9.
- [Approved] ELITES DO NOT CHANGE (Jess, 2026-11-05). An Elite inherits the wild rules and keeps its current differences as mods on top (status cut and short trap, knockback immunity, no Fissure KO, and the rest), so a later change to wild entities reaches it automatically. Damage: Player entities use the player attack and defense calculations, Wild entities the wild ones.
- [Approved] HOST MODEL (Jess, 2026-11-05). Every entity is a Strong Host or a Weak Host.
  - Strong Host: follows ALL the player's rules. Player, Gym Leader, Uber, World Boss. A gym leader can do anything a player can (moves of any shape, every card including team cards, items, dodge, a team that swaps like the player's); the designer then RESTRICTS by hand where wanted. Default allow, authored restrictions (for example the self-heal cap per fight, tells). This replaces the 2026-10-31 allow-list model (a fixed set of move forms, no team cards).
  - Weak Host: follows the wild enemy rules. Elite, Wild Enemy. An Elite is a wild enemy scaled up in size and stats, plus its own added rules; it is not a strong host with restrictions.
  - Gym tells (wind-up before an attack) stay for now as an authored restriction on the leader's AI; they may be removed later.
- [Legacy behavior] Until the host-model slice (docs/proposals/host-model-plan.md, audit: docs/proposals/host-model-audit.md), the rules below that say "strong hosts" use the OLD meaning: boss, elite, gym leader, never the player (js/systems/status.js strong(), SS13, the 25% freeze and sleep cut, the trap root). Elites keep these rules as their own mods, and leaders follow the player's rules with their authored restrictions (slice 6).

### Trap, enemies

- [Approved] Trap on an enemy is a root: movement multiplier 0 while trapped. Wild enemies: 2 s. Strong hosts: 25% of that, no extension, then a 10 s no-reapply window. (QA-10; CP1-Q5; js/systems/status.js CONTROL.trap.root, STRONG)
- [Approved] Ghost types are immune to trap. (archive 2026-10-19 Status Slice C; js/systems/status.js CONTROL.trap)
- [Approved] Fire Spin applies burn at the full standard length and trap, and does not strip. Its persistent orbit damage stays as authored. The full ruling lives in docs/MOVE-INTENT.md. (QA-10; Q17)
- History (removed in slice 7b, 2026-11-01): E1 (archive 2026-10-20 Status Slice E1), "Enemies cannot be trapped any more", held only on the legacy path, where no move carried a trap. Verified gone: the Fire Spin and trap-mine scenarios trap enemies on the only path (move-intent harness).
- History (removed in slice 7b-2a, 2026-11-01): the legacy Fire Spin stripped every beneficial stat stage and burned for half the normal length (`strip`, `burnHalf`). (archive 2026-10-20 Status Slice E1)

### Trap, player

- [Approved] TRAP on the player = no damage, no slow: manual team swaps are locked for 8 s (faint auto-swap still works). (archive 2026-10-20 Status Slice E1; js/systems/status.js:10 and CONTROL.trap.dur)
- This rule is separate from the enemy rule above and does not change with it.

### Other status effects

- [Approved] Wild enemies: freeze 2 s, then a 4 s refreeze window; a Fire hit thaws it, never the same instant; Ice immune. Sleep 2 to 6 s, damage never wakes. Paralysis 10 s, x0.5 movement, 25% of attacks fizzle, Electric immune. Confusion 2 to 8 s, wander + 50% fizzle. A turn is 2 s. (archive 2026-10-19 Status Slice C)
- [Approved] Strong hosts: freeze and sleep last 25% as long, then a 10 s no-reapply window. Paralysis, slow, trap (except the root above) and confusion work at full strength. No blanket boss immunity. (archive 2026-10-19 Status Slice C; BOSS-REWORK section 3c)
- [Approved] Trapped and paralysed enemies still attack. Frozen and sleeping ones do not move or attack. (archive 2026-10-19 Status Slice C)
- [Approved] HARD CC ON THE PLAYER: every timer is FULL (no 25% cut). After a hard CC (sleep, freeze, paralysis) ends there is a 2.5 s grace with no new hard CC (TUNE). Major statuses still compete (one at a time). (archive 2026-10-20 Status Slice E1)
- [Approved] SHAKE OFF (player): each tap takes 0.25 s off the most restrictive mashable status, floor 0.5 s remaining, at most 12 taps per second. Mashable: sleep, freeze, paralysis, confusion, trap, daze. Not mashable: burn, poison, toxic, drowsy, slow, stat drops. (archive 2026-10-20 Status Slice E1)
- [Approved] AUTO-SHAKE (accessibility setting, default off) taps Shake off at 3 taps per second while anything is shakeable; each tap has the normal effect. (archive 2026-10-20 Status Slice E2e)
- [Approved] DAZE = movement heading jitters up to +-35 degrees, re-rolled every 0.2 to 0.4 s and eased, 6 s, no fizzle. SLOW = Speed -1 stage for 6 s. HASTE = a nova that gives allies Speed +1 for 6 s, stacking to 2; the caster is excluded, bosses and gym leaders skipped. DROWSY (8 s) multiplies every sleep-effect chance by 1.5, capped at 100%. (archive 2026-10-20 Status Slice E1) SLOW and HASTE stay outside the stat stage tracker model ("Stat stages", carrier statuses).
- [Approved] FLINCH: chance from the move's PokeAPI flinch value, one roll per move per target per 0.5 s. EVERYONE can flinch (wild, elites, bosses, gym leaders), with no window and no lockout. Frozen or asleep units cannot flinch. The lost action, recoil and freeze rules are in the source entry. (archive 2026-10-19 Status Slice D2)
- [Approved] FLINCH ON THE PLAYER (a gym leader's move with a flinch chance, Bite): one roll per move per 0.5 s. On a flinch, every cooldown move in the player's ACTIVE group restarts its full cooldown (a bonded group is hit together), then holds 0.3 s before it counts. Pure buffs keep casting, persistent moves keep running, shapes already out continue, inactive groups are untouched. The player recoils like any unit: moving, back half its body at 2x its speed, then still 0.2 to 0.3 s; standing, still only. Input is ignored during it. No flinch while asleep or frozen. A "Flinched" popup shows. (Jess 2026-11-01; G.MoveSim.flinch, Player.applyFlinch)
- [Approved] RIDERS ONTO THE PLAYER: a gym leader's hit lands its whole payload. Ailments go through G.Status with the player's rules (full timers, one major, the hard-CC grace, berries, Shake off); burn, poison and toxic are the player's damage over time. Foe stat drops land on the player's own tracker on the leader's Strong-host Gate and Duration ("Stat stages" SS13). The leader's own raises (Metal Claw) and drain heal (Giga Drain, half of what it dealt) land on the leader. Hex doubles against a major ailment. Knockback is skipped (the player has none). (Jess 2026-11-01)
- [Approved] BENCH: every per-Pokemon effect (statuses, stages, DoT, own drops) KEEPS TICKING on the bench; only skill cooldown does not tick. A benched Pokemon can faint to DoT; that shows a red toast. (archive 2026-10-20 Status Slice E2a rulings and E2b)
- [Approved] FAINT AND REVIVE: fainting and revive clear every status, DoT and own drop. The shared TEAM raises stay. (archive 2026-10-20 Status Slice E2b)
- [Approved] FREEZE AND SLEEP DROP THE PLAYER'S ORBS AND PULSES: while the player is stunned, the active orbit move clears its orbs, trail patches stop pulsing and turrets stop firing; placed mines and meteors still go off. (archive 2026-10-20 Status Slice E2d)

## Damage over time

- [Approved] DoT is a fraction of the target's MAX HP per turn: burn 1/16, poison 1/8, Toxic (badly poisoned) N/16 on turn N, N capped at 15. ## Stat stages

Formerly "Stat stages and maxStacks"; a pointer to that title means this section. SS1 to SS13 were approved by Jess on 2026-10-30 (DECISIONS 2026-10-30, stat stage model). Rationale, the pokeapi shape counts, the worked examples and the design intent: docs/proposals/stat-stage-model.md (history; its R1 to R13 are SS1 to SS13 here, and this text wins). Built on the new path in slice 5a (2026-10-31). The two team cards (SS10) landed in slice 5b; the legacy path's own model was removed in slice 7b (2026-11-01).

- [Approved] Mainline stat STAGES for Attack, Defense, Sp. Atk, Sp. Def and Speed, on every host (player, wild, elite, boss, gym leader). Accuracy and evasion are skipped. (archive 2026-10-19 Status Slice D1) [Approved] code differs, see TODO: accuracy and evasion stages join the tracker model for the Kanto imports (Jess, 2026-11-01): a hit's chance is 3/(3+n) for a net evasion-over-accuracy of n stages (mainline table), a miss deals nothing and applies no rider. Per-move rulings: MOVE-INTENT (double-team, sand-attack and the rest).
- [Approved] The CAP is the +-6 ladder limit: a stat's stage on a host is the sum of its trackers (SS6), clamped to +-6. The maximum stage can never exceed 6 for any single stat. (A.2; archive Slice D1)
- [Approved] STAGE LADDERS: each stat has ONE base number B. A raise of s stages multiplies by (B + s) / B, a drop by B / (B + |s|), the same ladder for raises and drops, on every host. Attack, Defense, Sp. Atk and Sp. Def start at the mainline B = 2; Speed starts at B = 4. Each stat's B is tuned separately; current values: see js/systems/stages.js BASE. (archive 2026-10-20 Status Slice E2a; combat-architecture D30)
- [Approved] SCOPE: every raise is TEAM-WIDE; every drop (enemy-cast or self-cast) is PER POKEMON. The stage of a stat on the player = team raises + own drops, clamped to +-6. (archive 2026-10-20 Status Slice E2a)
- [Approved] WHERE IT IS READ: Attack stage multiplies the attacker factor of PHYSICAL moves only, Sp. Atk stage special moves only, status moves neither. Defense / Sp. Defense stage multiplies the defender's Def / SpD in the damage ratio. The Speed stage multiplies the FINAL movement speed and is not bound by the species-identity shaping; it multiplies with paralysis and trap. (archive 2026-10-20 Status Slice E2a; archive Slice D1 ruling)
- [Approved] Foe drops have no per-move cap: they climb by SS4 up to the Cap. Strong hosts: SS13. (A.2; Q16)

Terms: **stages** = what one success gives, straight from pokeapi (Swords Dance +2, Harden +1, Leaf Storm -2, Bubble Beam -1). **Cap** = the +-6 limit above. **Gate** = the minimum time between two climbs of one tracker (not the "cast gate" in GLOSSARY.md). **Duration** = how long a tracker holds after its last success.

- SS1 [Approved] ONE TRACKER PER (Pokemon, move, stat) on each recipient: its current stages, its Gate timer and its Duration timer. Each Pokemon counts separately (two roster members with Swords Dance are two trackers). A move with several stat entries (Dragon Dance, Shell Smash) has one tracker per stat.
- SS2 [Approved] WHO ROLLS: one roll per recipient, per use. A change on a foe rolls once per enemy hit. A change on the caster or its team rolls once per cast, on the first enemy hit of that cast (a cast that hits nothing rolls nothing). A non-damaging move rolls once per cast. A persistent or rehit move counts each rehit tick as a use. Reads the existing `side` field; no new field.
- SS3 [Approved] FIRST SUCCESS applies the move's pokeapi stages immediately and starts both timers.
- SS4 [Approved] EVERY SUCCESS resets Duration. A success while the Gate is open also CLIMBS: it adds the move's pokeapi stages again and restarts the Gate.
- SS5 [Approved] A Duration window with no success sets that tracker's stages to 0. Only that tracker; other moves on the same stat are untouched.
- SS6 [Approved] A stat's stage on a host = the sum of all trackers on it (team raises plus own drops for a player, as in SCOPE above), clamped to the Cap. Each tracker's own stages are also clamped to +-6, so one move cannot bank stages past the Cap and hide another tracker's reset. (Jess, 2026-10-30)
- SS7 [Approved] BASE TIMERS, global, same for every move: Gate 6.0 s, Duration 6.1 s. Duration is always a little longer than Gate so a move that succeeds every Gate never drops. The gap is at least 0.1 s, not 0.001 s: a frame can be up to 1/30 s, so a smaller gap is decided by update order. This replaces TEAM_GRACE's refresh-instead-of-stack behavior.
- SS8 [Approved] Pure buff moves (no damage) have no cooldown of their own: they cast when their Gate opens. Damaging moves keep their own cooldown, which only decides how often they roll. Strong hosts: see SS13. A pure buff whose cast fizzles waits one Gate before it tries again. (Jess, 2026-10-30) A pure buff casts only while its group is the active group ("Swap rule": only the active group fires), so a buff alone in its slot climbs only while it is active and its stages carry onto the next group for the rest of their Duration (charge, then swap). The swap rule's "the swapped-in move fires at once" applies to a pure buff only when that move has no Gate running (no tracker, or its Gate is open); with a Gate running it waits for the Gate to open, then casts and climbs. Swapping back after its tracker has reset starts the climb over. (Jess, 2026-10-30)
- SS9 [Approved] Raises are team-wide (unchanged). A benched Pokemon cannot cast, so its trackers stop refreshing and end one Duration after their last success (the bench keeps ticking: "Swap rule" above and BENCH in "Other status effects"). Swap juggling can reach the Cap a few seconds sooner for a fraction of a second; the Cap bounds it.
- SS10 [Approved] UPGRADES: two TEAM-WIDE cards, not per Pokemon and not per move: "Stat Stage Gate -x%" (shorter Gate, faster climbs) and "Stat Stage Duration +x%" (longer Duration, easier to hold). They apply to every stat change the team causes, wherever it lands: raises on the team, drops on foes, AND drops on itself (Leaf Storm climbs faster and lasts longer). That double edge is intended (Brotato-style trade-off, survivor-like-design-reference principle 6). Wild entities have no cards and use the base timers; a Player-entity leader may hold both cards when authored (LEADER PRINCIPLE, Jess 2026-11-07; built in entity model slice 6). Step size x is a slice 6 number, small, since one team card equals about six per-Pokemon picks. No card raises a move's stages. Code differs, see TODO: the two cards are built in slice 5b with the team-wide card rework (Jess, 2026-10-30; the team-wide upgrades branch is folded into 5b).
- SS11 [Approved] Chance moves (N = 1): a chance move holds while it lands at least one success per Duration window. Hold probability per window = 1 - (1 - p)^k, k = rolls in the window.
- SS12 [Approved] SELF DROPS CLIMB ON A LONGER GATE: a drop a Pokemon puts on itself (the 13 damaging self-drop moves plus Shell Smash's drops) uses Self-drop Gate = Gate x SELF_DROP_GATE_MUL, with SELF_DROP_GATE_MUL = 2 (one tunable constant, slice 6). Duration is unchanged (6.1 s), so the drop still ends 6.1 s after the last hit. The team Gate card scales both Gates proportionally; the Duration card lengthens self drops too, so both cards keep their double edge. No per-move authoring.
- SS13 [Approved] STRONG HOSTS FOLLOW THE MODEL: bosses, elites and gym leaders (old meaning, see Host model above: the host list becomes Player, Gym Leader, Uber, World Boss) use SS1 to SS12 with no per-move exception. Stat changes a strong host causes (its own raises, drops it puts on itself or on the player) use Strong-host Gate = Gate x STRONG_HOST_GATE_MUL, with STRONG_HOST_GATE_MUL = 1 (one tunable constant, slice 6; lower it if gym leaders feel soft). Duration is unchanged. Drops the player puts on a strong host use the team's timers like any foe; there is no strong-host stage-drop reduction (the strong-host reductions are freeze, sleep and trap only). This retires BOSS-REWORK 3c's "the move's stacks as the stage cap" for gym Harden. SS13 governs what a success does, not when a strong host casts. D-ENEMY ruled the scheduling (Jess, 2026-10-31): a gym leader's mon plays like the player, so SS8 applies to it (a pure buff casts the moment its Gate opens, with no tell; damaging moves keep their cooldowns), and a team-scope raise lands on the mon itself, since it has no team store. Built in slice 7a (BOSS-REWORK section 3d). A leader's team shares raises exactly as yours (Jess 2026-11-07, slice 7 Q5): team-wide, kept past a faint, ending one Duration after the last success; own drops clear at the faint ("Host model", LEADER TEAM).
- [Proposed] Bond tax (Jess, 2026-10-30): a move whose stat change HELPS its own side (a raise on the caster or team, a drop on a foe) taxes its bond group. Every move in the group, the taxing move included, gets Tax = 0.5 x Gate x chance added to its cooldown (chance as a fraction, 1 for a guaranteed change). A change that hurts its own side (a self drop such as Leaf Storm, a raise on a foe) is not taxed. Later moves will need patching for interactions (Jess).
