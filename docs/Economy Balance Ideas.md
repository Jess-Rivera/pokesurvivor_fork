Upgrade Point Rules

- Each Pokémon has a maximum capacity of 40 upgrade points.
- All Pokémon share the same upgrade catalog and point capacity.
- Pokémon-specific upgrades only affect the Pokémon that owns them.
- Run-wide upgrades stack across the roster and remain active regardless of the active Pokémon.
- Individual upgrades have five ranks (I-V).
- Run-wide upgrades have three ranks (I-III).
- Individual upgrade point costs are incremental: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 points per rank.
- Run-wide upgrade point costs are incremental: 5, 10, 15 points per rank.
- Upgrade points are purchased with gold using a progressive 40-point cost curve.
Catalog totals:
- 11 Pokémon-specific upgrades.
- 8 run-wide upgrades.
- 19 total upgrades.
