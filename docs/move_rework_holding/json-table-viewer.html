Filtering, sorting and shortcuts
Global search (top bar): words are ANDed across every field. -word excludes, "two words" is an exact phrase.
Column filters (second header row). Comma means OR.
  Numbers: 40 equals, >=60, <30, !=0, 10..50 range, >=40 <=90 (space = AND), 25,30 (OR), empty, !empty.
  Text: burn contains, =fire exact, ^fl starts with, ing$ ends with, !poison does not contain, /reg(ex)?/ regex, empty, !empty. Positive terms are ORed, negative terms are all required.
  True/false columns: true or false.
Sorting: click a header to sort (click again for descending, again to clear). Shift+click adds a secondary sort.
Columns: pick them under Columns; drag header cells to reorder; drag a header edge to resize. Your layout is remembered per file shape.
Rows: click a row to open its full record. ↑↓ move between rows, Esc closes. / focuses search.
