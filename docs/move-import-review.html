Move Import Review

Every PokeAPI move that some Pokémon can learn, with a proposed Kind, what is missing before it plays exactly like mainline, and the effects it carries. Approve, flag or skip each one. Decisions save as you go, and Claude can read them back.

What "ready" means. A damaging move can be imported today with its secondary effects stored but dormant, the same way the 64 moves in the game already carry them. The tier says what has to be built before the effect actually fires. Kind is your call. The proposed Kind now comes from a few-shot pass: Claude classified each move from its PokeAPI data and prose, using your 64 hand-set moves as worked examples. Where the old rule classifier disagrees, the row says so, and the Agreement filter lets you review those first. Confidence 1 means a guess. Second pass: after your audit, the 63 moves that had no Kind or were trap moves were redone (trap moves are Mine; status moves that act on yourself are Buff, ones that act on a foe are Control), so the 50 audited moves are unchanged. Third pass: your 99 reviews were used as new examples. Unique status moves that copy, swap or rewrite a foe's attributes became boss-only Projectile, hazards became Mine, and 30 unreviewed moves changed (filter: Revised in re-pass). Reviewed moves never change.
